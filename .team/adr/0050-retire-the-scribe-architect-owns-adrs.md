# ADR-0050: Retire the Scribe and its briefs; the Architect owns the ADRs

**Date:** 2026-10-13 (s114)  
**Scope:** runner (`roles`, `config`, `state`, `router`, `dispatch`, `gates`, `cli`), built-in role files, dashboard, SKILL.md, protocol.md  
**Design:** `agile-team/docs/design/s114.md` (SC1–SC12)  
**Supersedes:** every earlier ADR that routes ADR writing to the Scribe (e.g. s108's ADR-0041 amendment row, now the Architect's)

## Context

After each done story the Scribe ran (Haiku, low effort). It wrote ADRs and
rewrote one `.team/briefs/<role>.md` per role, and `RoleBook.system_prompt`
pasted that file into every prompt as "Brief from the Scribe". This went
wrong in three ways:

- The briefs were second-hand and often stale. For example, "docs due"
  lists stayed in them after the work was done, so roles acted on old
  summaries instead of reading the story, design, ADRs and code.
- It cost one step per story, and every step's prompt carried the brief's
  tokens.
- The ADRs were written after the fact, by a role that had not made the
  decisions and did not know why they were made.

## Decision

1. **No briefs.** A system prompt is `_shared` + the role chain + addendum +
   charter + file scope. Nothing reads `.team/briefs/`, and `roles.BRIEFS`
   is gone (SC1). Step briefs (the runner's standard brief and the PO's
   `run_role` brief) do not change.
2. **No Scribe role or routing.** The built-in `scribe.md`,
   `RunState.scribe_due`, the router's Scribe picker and the dispatch queue
   are deleted. A story that reaches `done` queues nothing.
   `UNGATED_ROLES` and `BOUNDARY_UNGATED` now hold only the Manager (SC2,
   SC3).
3. **A retired role is data.** `roles.RETIRED_ROLES` maps
   `"scribe"` → the reason, and it is the only place that names the retired
   role. On load:
   - old `state.json` drops `scribe_due` (`state.RUN_DROPPED`) and drops
     overrides for retired roles (SC4);
   - a `[roles.scribe]` section raises `ConfigError` (SC6);
   - a `scribe.md` role file that would still shape the role (a replace or an
     addendum) raises `RoleError`, and `cli.main` prints it as a one-line
     refusal (SC7, SC8);
   - a comments-only stub is ignored, so repos with a scaffolded stub keep
     working.
4. **The Architect owns the ADRs.** Its built-in `write` is
   `["{docs}", ".team/adr/**"]`. At design it writes
   `.team/adr/NNNN-<slug>.md` (Context, Decision, Consequences), numbered
   one above the highest ADR there, for each decision it makes. When a
   decision changes, it amends the existing ADR with a dated
   "Amended by <story>" section and does not write a new one that
   contradicts it (SC9).

## Consequences

- Each story takes one step fewer, and every prompt is shorter. Roles read
  first-hand sources.
- The role that makes a decision writes its ADR, at the time it knows why.
- Repos that have a `[roles.scribe]` section or a non-stub `scribe.md` must
  remove it before their next `start`. The refusal text says what to do.
- Repos that override `[roles.architect] write` must add `.team/adr/**`, or
  the Architect cannot write ADRs there. This repo's `.agile-team.toml` was
  updated in s114.
- Old event logs and ledgers that name `scribe` still render. The dashboard
  sorts unknown roles last. An old `interrupted` or `in_flight` record for
  `scribe` still loads, but `run_role("scribe")` is refused as an unknown
  role.
- The older `docs/adr/ADR-NNN-*.md` series stays as it is. New ADRs go to
  `.team/adr/` only.

## Amended at s114 design review

The dashboard fixture's curated part cannot be regenerated by its script.
`starting_point()` keeps the committed file, and `add_live()` rebuilds only
the events after `CURATED_END`. So the Scribe's curated entries were removed
by hand, as whole entries. This does not change the rule that no field
inside an entry is hand-edited to make a test pass.
