## User story

As the human, I want the Customer Proxy to be truly walled off from source and
tests, not only for the word forms s7 checks, so that acceptance reflects what
a customer can see.

## Notes

NARROWED (t101): the human replaced the original ACs with these three. The story
proves the behaviour with automated tests. A test that passes closes the story.
A test that fails is fixed in the same story.

The original story: pre-existing gaps P1, P2 and P4 from the s7 review
(.team/reviews/s7.md), plus the architect's s7 redesign note: `$VAR`/`$(…)`/`~`,
wildcard absolute repo paths, and cwd changes (`cd` with no argument, `cd /`,
`cd {..,}`) all get past the token check. P3 (an installed package's source
readable in the sandbox) is inherent to the `package` delivery and is out of
scope unless the human decides otherwise.
