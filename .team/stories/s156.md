  - id: AC1

      When nothing in the Sprint Backlog can move, the runner hands the PO the stall

  - id: AC2
    environment: cli
    text: >-
      If the Sprint Backlog is still stuck after 3 hand-overs with no PO action that changes
      a Sprint Backlog story (continue, release, run_role, revert, a cancel request or a
      story request), the runner sets `blocked` with the reason "the Sprint Backlog is
      stuck" and each story's stall reason, and posts it to the liaison.
  - id: AC3
    environment: cli
    text: >-
      Under `until-blocker`, refusing `notify_user` `done` or `blocked` looks only at the

      Product Backlog stories are ready.
  - id: AC4
    environment: cli
    text: >-

In sprint 45, after the human chose to keep only s151, s152 and s153, the PO was made to refill the sprint from the Product Backlog every idle pass (s150, s10, s12, s138, s144) while a test cascade blocked every gate, and the run halted twice with "the PO did not start a sprint or add ready stories".

## User story

As the human, I want a stuck Sprint Backlog reported with its reasons rather than refilled from the Product Backlog, so the sprint I chose stays the sprint the team works on and a blocker reaches me instead of being buried under new work.
