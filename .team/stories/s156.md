acceptance:
  - id: AC1
    environment: cli
    text: >-
      When nothing in the Sprint Backlog can move, the runner hands the PO the stall
      reasons for each Sprint Backlog story and offers no Product Backlog stories. The
      `ready_backlog` hand-over and its advice to `add_to_sprint` are gone.
  - id: AC2
    environment: cli
    text: >-
      If the Sprint Backlog is still stuck after 3 hand-overs with no PO action that changes
      a Sprint Backlog story (continue, release, run_role, revert, a cancel request or a
      story request), the runner sets `blocked` with the reason "the Sprint Backlog is
      stuck" and each story's stall reason, and posts it to the liaison.
  - id: AC3
    environment: cli
    text: >-
      Under `until-blocker`, refusing `notify_user` `done` or `blocked` looks only at the
      Sprint Backlog: it is refused while a Sprint Backlog story can move, never because
      Product Backlog stories are ready.
  - id: AC4
    environment: cli
    text: >-
      At a sprint end, the `next_sprint` hand-over still lists ready Product Backlog stories
      for the PO to plan the next sprint, unchanged.
  - id: AC5
    environment: cli
    text: >-
      `add_to_sprint` still works, with its reason, as a deliberate PO action. No runner
      hand-over or role text suggests it as the answer to a stuck Sprint Backlog.
  - id: AC6
    environment: cli
    text: >-
      Runner names, hand-over texts and events say Product Backlog or Sprint Backlog, never

  - id: AC7

  - id: AC8

## User story

As the human, I want a stuck Sprint Backlog reported with its reasons rather than refilled from the Product Backlog, so the sprint I chose stays the sprint the team works on and a blocker reaches me instead of being buried under new work.
