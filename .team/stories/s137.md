As the human watching the Flow chart, I want a running role's box to count its
open step, so the box does not read `0 · $0.00 · 0s` while the role works.

Source: the human's message t100 (story J, a bug; screenshot: the running
architect box on s131, design, 2.7m, 156k tokens/min, showing
`opus · 0 · $0.00 · 0s`). Asked for by the human; ACs written by the liaison (t100) (sub-bullets joined
into one line each).

Cause, found by the liaison in `agile_team/dashboard/index.html`:
- `roleNumbers` (L1225) reads `ctx.totals` from `scopeTotals` (L832).
- `scopeTotals` sums the ledger (`scopeEntries`, written when a step ends) and
  the times of finished steps only (`scopeSteps` → `addStepTimes`, L841).
- So a running step adds nothing until it ends. In Story scope at the start of
  a story, its only step is the running one, so the box reads 0.
