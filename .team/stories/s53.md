# s53: The PO's story admin tools: hold, release, request a cancel, and change the acceptance delivery

**Delivery:** cli (the Customer Proxy verifies it through `agile-team status` and the PO tools; the board labels come with s83's tab).

## User story

As the PO, I want tools to hold and release a story, to ask the human to cancel one, and to correct the delivery the Customer Proxy uses to accept it, each with a reason, so the run order and the board stay true without anyone editing `state.json` by hand. Cancelling stays the human's decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1. Hold.** `hold_story(story, reason)` holds a story that isn't done or cancelled.
  - The reason is required and must not be empty.
  - The router skips the story, and it keeps its position.
  - The board shows `⏸ PO hold: <reason>`, not "needs human".
  - No question is created.
  - Holding an already-held story replaces the reason. Both reasons are in the events.
- **AC2. Release.** `release_story(story, reason)` releases a PO hold, or a `blocked` story with no hold and no open question, to the step it stopped at, with status active.
  - A story with an open question is refused, and the refusal names the question id ("answer m<N> instead").
  - The tool never reports success while the story is still held or blocked. A test pins this with s4's case: blocked, no hold, no question.
  - `continue_story` is unchanged.
- **AC3. Cancel is the human's decision.** No agent can cancel a story.
  - **Request:** `request_cancel(story, reason)`, the PO's tool, holds the story as `⏸ cancel requested: <reason>` (as in AC1). It also asks the human one question, naming the story and the reason, with the options "cancel" and "keep".
  - **Confirm:** only an answer of "cancel" to that question cancels the story. The runner then:
    - takes the story out of `state.order` and out of its sprint;
    - sets its status to `cancelled`;
    - keeps its story file and history.

    The code checks that the cancel comes from the answer to that question id. No tool argument or role step can do it.
  - **Keep:** an answer of "keep" releases the hold, and the story carries on.
  - **Other agents:** a role or the Manager that thinks a story should go tells the PO, and the PO uses `request_cancel`. Nothing else changes until the human answers.
  - **Afterwards:** the router never routes a cancelled story, and the board and Backlog tab label it "cancelled". A done story can't be requested. A story with commits keeps them, and the request's reason must say what happens to its code.
  - **First uses** after this ships, already confirmed by the human: s80, s50, s18 and s54. The liaison relays those confirmations.
- **AC4. Change the acceptance delivery.** `change_acceptance_delivery(story, delivery)` changes which configured `[[delivery]]` (`cli`, `dashboard`, `unattended`) the Customer Proxy uses to try an unfinished story.
  - The name gets the same check as `open_story` (`Config.delivery_refusal`), with the same refusal text.
  - Nothing changes on a refusal.
- **AC5. One shape for all four.**
  - An unknown story id is refused.
  - Each success records one event (the tool, the story, the reason, and the before and after values) and saves the state.
  - The tools are rows in `po_tools.SCHEMAS`, sharing one refusal helper. There's no branch per tool.
- **AC6. The PO's role file** says when to use each tool:
  - hold instead of a parked `ask_user`;
  - `request_cancel` whenever anyone, the PO included, thinks a story should go, and never treat a story as cancelled before the human confirms;
  - `change_acceptance_delivery` after a "no delivery configured" refusal.
- **AC7. Tests pin these cases:**
  - releasing s4's case;
  - `request_cancel` only holds the story and asks; the story is cancelled only after a "cancel" answer to that question, and a "keep" answer releases it;
  - a cancelled story is skipped by the router after a resume;
  - a refused `change_acceptance_delivery` leaves the state unchanged;
  - a hold creates no question.

Docs move with the code: `agile-team/SKILL.md`, `agile-team/reference/protocol.md` and the README row list the tools.
