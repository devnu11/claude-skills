# s53: The PO can hold and release a story with a reason

**Delivery:** agile-team runner (po_tools, state, router, dashboard).

## User story

As the PO, I want a tool to hold a story, or release it, with a reason shown on the board, so I can run one story at a time without parking an open question that nobody should answer.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A PO tool `hold_story(story, reason)` holds a story in the sprint. The router skips it (P4 keeps its position). The reason is required and non-empty.
2. The board shows the hold on the card as `⏸ <reason>`, labelled as a PO hold, not "needs human". No question is created and nothing is added to the human's question list.
3. `continue_story` releases a PO hold, as for any other hold; its note reaches the next step's brief.
4. `hold_story` on an unknown or done story is refused with the reason. A hold on a story already held keeps the first reason or replaces it by a rule stated in the design, with a test.
5. The hold is recorded as a dashboard event (story, reason, who).
6. Tool schema in `po_tools.SCHEMAS`; the product-owner role file, SKILL.md and protocol.md document it, and the role file says to use it in place of a parked `ask_user` for a story that waits on priority.

## Gap found 2026-10-11

The PO also needs a way to un-block a story that is `blocked` with no hold and no question (s4 was stuck that way, see s52). Either `continue_story` really releases it to the step it was blocked at, or a `release_story` tool does, with a reason. It must never answer "continued" while the story stays blocked. Add an acceptance test for this.

## Origin

2026-10-11: with one story movable at a time, the PO holds eight stories with an open question (m49) that nobody should answer until s38 is done. It clutters the human's questions and makes the board say "needs human".
