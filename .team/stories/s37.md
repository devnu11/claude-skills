  - id: AC2
    text: >-
      `next_wake` is null whenever the job is running. The offline path (the
      supervisor's `_reconnect`, in `unattended/runner/unattended/supervisor.py`) and
      the resume that follows clear it. An offline wait reports no wake time: it waits
      for the network, not for a time. Seen live: job agile-team-dashboard-4 resumed at
      14:13 CDT after a session limit, went offline at 15:05 ("API server
      unreachable"), came back at 16:20, and `status` still reported "status":
      "running" with "next_wake": "2026-10-10T14:13:00-05:00".
  - id: AC3
    text: >-
      The reset time comes from the runner's own halt line (the `agile-team start: ...
      resets ...; run \`agile-team start --resume\`` line), or from a structured source
      such as state.json's `status_reason`, in that order of preference, over free
      text in the output. The word "resets" in the PO's own output is never read as a
      reset time. Seen live: at 17:26 CDT the runner halted with "session limit,
      resets 9pm (America/Chicago)"; unattended's reason became "usage limit, resets
      the count) and add AC4b ...", matched from the PO's output in the last 50 lines.
      With no time it could parse it fell back to a 5 hour wait (until 22:26) instead
      of waking at 21:03, and the team sat idle for about 1.5 hours until the liaison
      used `resume --now`.
  - id: AC4
    text: >-
      A reason with no parseable time does not count as a stated reset. It falls back
      to the documented default wait, and the status says that the wake time is a
      fallback, not a stated reset.
  - id: AC5
    text: >-
      A test uses this exact output (the halt line plus the PO text containing
      "resets the count") and expects 21:03 America/Chicago. Another test covers the
      offline wait and resume with `next_wake` null throughout. Coverage stays at or
      above 95%, and ruff check and ruff format are clean.

      supervisor is re-pinned: the job is supervised by an `unattended` running from
      another worktree. The story says so in its hand-off to the human.

## User story

As the human running the team unattended, I want a resume to wake at the real
reset time and `status` to tell the truth after an offline wait, so that the
team is never idle for hours on a wrong clock.

## Notes

The human wants the team to fix this, not the liaison, "so we don't have to
deal with merge conflicts", and near the top of the stack.
