# s49: Keep the PO's story order across sprint start and resume

**Delivery:** agile-team runner (state and priority).

## User story

As the PO, I want the order I set with `start_sprint` or `prioritise` to stay in `state.order` until I change it, so the router does not fall back to creation order.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Find why `state.order` was `[]` in sprint 17 although `start_sprint` listed the stories in priority order (design s40 P7, P8 say it should hold the whole queue). Candidates: a later `start_sprint` or `begin_sprint` clears it, a resume or reload drops it (P1 legacy load gives `[]`), or the pinned runner (81306a4) predates the code.
2. A regression test fails on the cause found and passes after the fix.
3. After `start_sprint`, a resume, a story going done and a `continue_story`, `state.order` still holds every unfinished story in the order set.

## Origin

The liaison saw `state.order == []` in sprint 17 after m44 said the sprint started in priority order. The PO re-set it with `prioritise` on 2026-10-11.
