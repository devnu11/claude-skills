## User story

As the human, I want a story that is already implemented to move past the tests
gate, and the Manager to be able to advance a stuck story, so that the pipeline
does not stall on a demand no role can meet.

## Scope ruling (2026-10-11)

The Manager rescoped s46 at the round cap. The built-in role files
`unit-tester.md` and `manager.md` are outside the developer's file scope, so
their edits (design/s46.md "Role files (built-in)") are NOT part of s46. They
move to follow-up story s54. The Architect trims the "Role files" section of
the design to a pointer to s54; the developer does the runner code, tests
named in the design and protocol docs only.
