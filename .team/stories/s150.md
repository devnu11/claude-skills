Scope: the agile-team dashboard (`dashboard/index.html`, its node-harness tests).

## User story

As the human, I want a Board card's wait pill to stay small, so the Board stays readable
when a wait has a long reason.

## What happens

`storyCard` (`agile-team/runner/agile_team/dashboard/index.html`, about L2459) builds the
pill as `⏸ ${s.wait.title}`, and the title is the reason plus its full detail
(`waits._title`). `.story .pill { white-space: normal; }` (about L268) lets it wrap, so a
long cancel reason becomes a seven-line rounded blob. The same detail is already on the
card in the "waits on" line (`waitLine`, about L2447), so it shows twice.

## Origin

A bug the human found on the Board on 2026-10-15 (screenshot of s57's card): "the pill
around the cancelled job includes the multiline description". Asked for by the human;
ACs written by the liaison (t119). Goes first in the next sprint, not in sprint 44.
