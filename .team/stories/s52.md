# s52: The board says "needs human" only when a question to the human is open

**Delivery:** agile-team runner (state, router, dashboard).

## User story

As the human, I want the board to say "needs human" only when a question is waiting for me, and to show the real reason otherwise, so I never hunt for a question that does not exist.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A card shows "needs human" only when at least one open question to the human lists that story. Otherwise it shows the real reason: a runner fault, a gate bounce with its reason, a hold with its reason, or waiting on priority.
2. A story must not sit `blocked` with no hold and no open question. The state is refused in code: whatever blocks a story (a gate, the runner, a ruling) records a hold reason at the same time, or the story is not set to `blocked`. A story found in that state on load is flagged (a dashboard event and a line in `agile-team status`), not left silent.
3. The board shows the hold or fault reason on the card, including the last gate reason for a story that bounced.
4. Tests cover each reason on the card and the refused state. Coverage stays at 95%+; ruff clean.
5. SKILL.md, protocol.md and the s40 design's board rules (W1, W2) are updated with the code.

## Gap found 2026-10-11

The PO had no tool to un-block s4. `continue_story` on a blocked story answered "continued" but left it `blocked` (no hold, no question), so it stayed unmovable and the router took s43 instead. The liaison released it by hand. The release path must either work or refuse with a reason; it must never report "continued" with the status unchanged.

Nothing was waiting on the human. It is the same class as the silent stall.
