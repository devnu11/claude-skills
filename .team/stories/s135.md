As the human, I want the liaison, who has the most context of the ask, to write
each story and its ACs, with the runner validating them and committing them in
the team's own history, so stories reach the team exactly as agreed and are
never lost to a reset.

Source: the human's message t99 (story H; plan:
`.team/run/liaison/plans/liaison-writes-stories-clean.md`). It replaces t97's
version. The ACs are the human's, verbatim (sub-bullets joined into one line
each).
