  - id: AC1
    text: >-
      Recording an opinion. The liaison records an opinion on a story with a CLI
      command (for example `agile-team opinion <story> <kind> <text>`), where `kind`
      is one of `keep`, `move-up`, `move-down`, `fold`, `close`, `scope` or `ask`.
      Opinions are data in the run directory, validated on write (unknown story or
      kind refused), and the latest opinion per story wins.
  - id: AC2
    text: >-
      Highlighting. In the Backlog tab, a story with a non-`keep` opinion shows a
      badge with its kind. The expanded story shows the opinion's text and date. A
      filter shows only the stories with suggestions.
  - id: AC3
    text: >-
      Asking for a review. An "Ask the liaison to review the backlog" button posts a
      request to the liaison inbox (story C, AC3) that wakes the liaison through the
      same `watch` trigger. The tab shows when the last review happened.
  - id: AC4
    text: >-
      Acting on one. The human can act on an opinion from the tab: dismiss it, or
      apply it. Applying a `move-up`, `move-down` or `close` goes through story B's
      reorder (or s53's cancel, once it exists), and applying any other kind posts it
      to the PO through story C. Either way it's recorded as an event.
  - id: AC5
    text: >-
      Tests cover: validation, the badge and filter, the review request trigger, and
      dismiss and apply.

## User story

As the human, I want to ask the liaison for its current opinion on the backlog
and see which stories it suggests changing, so I can review its recommendations
where the stories are.
