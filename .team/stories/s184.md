**User story.** As the liaison, I want "no further sprint" to stop the run once the PO has
said done. I don't want the runner to spend PO turns asking for sprint 53, then block, so
that I have to stop the job by hand.

**What happened (2026-10-25, end of sprint 52).** The liaison told the PO to start no
further sprint (t225/t226; e360359 brief "After sprint 52, no further sprint"). The PO
reverted s180 and notified `done` at 20:01:18 (m366, `tools.jsonl` status `ok`). That was
while the END boundary was running. The boundary's check passed, and the runner re-pinned
to ab91c1f2 (`runner-repin`, 20:03:29). It finished the boundary (e404695..e404697) and
handed the PO three `next_sprint` hand-overs (e404707, e404715, e404723). The PO answered:
"The runner keeps telling me to start sprint 53, and refuses `done` while Product Backlog
stories are ready … Each prompt costs a step" (m368). The runner then blocked with "The PO
did not start a sprint after 3 next-sprint hand-overs; ready in the Product Backlog: s57,
s141, s180, s6, s14, …" (m369). The liaison stopped the run with `agile-team stop` and
`unattended stop` (`decisions.md:564`).

**Cause (fact for the lost `done`: the code path is read at ab91c1f2 and the re-pin
event matches it; not reproduced. Fact for the hand-over text.)**
- No tool refused the PO's `done`. `KIND_REFUSALS["done"]` refuses only while a Sprint
  Backlog story can move (`po_tools.py:148-153`, `sprint_end.py:104-109`). The PO made no
  other done call after 20:01 (`tools.jsonl`). It read the refusal into the hand-over's
  advice: "don't notify done or blocked while Product Backlog stories are ready"
  (`sprint_end.py:21-25`, sent by `router.py:497-506`).
- The `done` status was overwritten. `notify_user` marks the status `done` but sets no
  `halted_by` (`po_tools.py:585-595`); the scheduler stops on `state.halted()`
  (`scheduler.py:187`). The passing end check set `state.repin`
  (`boundary_runner.py:466-469`). At the clean run end, `_repin_or` tests `halted_by` and
  `repin`, not the status, and marks the run `IDLE` whatever its status was
  (`cli.py:490-499`). The re-pinned process's `begin` marks it
  `RUNNING` (`scheduler.py:120`). The router then sees a finished sprint with cadence
  `until-blocker` and ready stories, and sends `NEXT_SPRINT` (`router.py:503-506`), up to
  `KEEP_GOING_CAP` = 3 and then `blocked` (`router.py:508-517`, `sprint_end.py:31-35`).
- (possible) Restarting with `--cadence sprint` would have halted at the sprint end
  (`state.py:151-155`). But the liaison's "no further sprint" reached the PO as text only,
  and nothing records it in the run's state.

**Placement:** Product Backlog. Files: `cli.py` (`_repin_or`), `scheduler.py` (`begin`),
`router.py` (`_sprint_end`, `_asked`), `sprint_end.py` (`NEXT_SPRINT_ADVICE`), and the
cadence and re-pin sections of `reference/protocol.md`. It relates to s128 (keep going
across sprint ends, self re-pin), which shipped the behaviour. Small.

ACs written by the liaison's drafting agent while the human was away; cross-checked by Fable (`reports/2026-10-25-review-gap-stories-2.md`). The human hasn't seen them.
