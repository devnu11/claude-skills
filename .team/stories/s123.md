Urgent bug, found by the liaison. `BoundaryRunner.after_fix` (`boundary_runner.py:201`) sets `b.hold = handover.hold_reason(report)`. That is the handover trigger names joined with ", " (right now `"stopped, questions"`), not a `BoundaryHold` value. `Boundary.__post_init__` (`boundary.py:259`) then fails on any load of `state.json`: `ValueError: 'stopped, questions' is not a valid BoundaryHold`. The sprint-42 boundary holds that value now. The liaison's `agile-team watch` crashes on it, and the runner will crash the next time it starts and loads the state (for example on a resume).

The t70 rule applies: the unit-tester writes the failing test first, then the developer fixes it. Do not touch `state.json` by hand; the liaison clears the bad value at the next stop.
