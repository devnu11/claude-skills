# DevOps reviews every design before tests (ops-review step)

## User story

As the human, I want DevOps to review every design before tests are written,
and to say what tooling the story's ACs need to be checked automatically, so
missing test harnesses, CI jobs and environments are caught at design, not
after acceptance.

In the pipeline, `ops-review` sits between `design` and `tests`.

## Out of scope

- Building the browser harness itself: that's A18, and AC3's `tooling` outcome is how stories will ask for it.

## Placement

The human asked that this story go **first in the next sprint** (not sprint 39).
