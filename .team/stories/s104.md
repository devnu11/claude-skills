# s104: Bug: the Flow chart's box labels pile up in the top-left corner and the boxes are empty

**Delivery:** dashboard

## What the human sees

Since the runner was re-pinned at 076308a (sprint 30's code), on http://localhost:8770/, Flow tab, Story scope (auto s96):

- every role box except the running one (architect) is empty;
- the labels of all the boxes are drawn on top of each other at the chart's top-left, unreadable ("idle", "sonnet · 0 · $0.00", "↩ 3 bounced" overlapping);
- the chart's text is tiny.

A possible cause, unconfirmed: Mermaid laid the chart out while the Flow tab was hidden, so every label measured 0×0 and sat at (0,0). Sprint 30 added the Backlog tab and changed rendering (s83, s84, s85). The developer finds the real cause first and says what it was in the handoff.

## User story

As the human, I want the Flow chart readable at all times, so I can see the team's state at a glance.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1 [dashboard].** Every box's label sits inside its box, in every scope (Story, Sprint, Run): on first load, after switching tabs and back, after a live update, and after the browser window is resized.
- **AC2 [dashboard].** No box is empty when its role has data for the scope. A role with none shows "idle" or "—" inside its own box.
- **AC3 [dashboard].** Text size isn't made worse. The chart's labels render at no less than they did before sprint 30, and the box stats at no less than 12 CSS px as rendered (font size × the SVG's scale). If the chart doesn't fit, it scrolls sideways rather than shrinking.
- **AC4 [dashboard].** A test under the node harness pins: each label's box is inside its node's box after a render that happens while the Flow tab is hidden and then shown, and the rendered size of the box stats. It fails on today's code.
