---
id: s162
title: "unattended status says how long a job has left before its deadline"
acceptance:
  - id: AC1
    environment: unattended
    text: >-
      `unattended status`, `unattended list` and `unattended wait` give each job a
      `time_left` beside its `deadline`: the time until the deadline in minutes, rounded
      down, written as `limits.span` writes durations (`23h41m`, `41m`, or `0m` under a
      minute). It's `overdue` once the deadline has passed, and `null` for a job with no
      deadline.
  - id: AC2
    environment: unattended
    text: >-
      `time_left` is computed against the clock when the command runs, from the job's
      stored `deadline`. No new state is written, and every other field of the output is
      as before.
---

**User story.** As the liaison watching a long run, I want `unattended status` to say how long a job has left, so I don't have to work it out from an ISO timestamp.

**Why now.** It's a deliberately small, independent story, to run in parallel with s161 in the first story-branches sprint: the human wants to see two stories run at once. It touches only `unattended/runner` (`cli.report`, with `limits_report` and `span`) and unattended's `SKILL.md` and `reference/protocol.md`. s161 doesn't touch those files; the only file the two might share is a row of `README.md`, on different lines (checked by Fable).

**Placement:** the human (2026-10-18): "can we put one other tiny story in there, so we can test having two stories in parallel". It goes in the next sprint, with s161.

ACs written by the liaison and cross-checked by Fable. The human approved the idea and the placement, not the AC text.
