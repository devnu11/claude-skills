title: Keep context small - fresh PO sessions at boundaries, per-step turn caps, and a leaner Unit Tester
delivery: cli
acceptance:
  - id: AC1
    text: >-
      The PO gets a fresh session at each boundary (sprint start, and after a story
      reaches done, unless the human picks otherwise). Before the old session ends, it
      writes a short handoff summary to `.team/po/handoff.md`: open decisions,
      pending questions with ids, what is next and why, and pointers to artifacts.
      The runner starts the next session from that summary, plus `story_status`, and
      saves the new session id at once. A boundary never drops an open question or a
      pending human message.
  - id: AC2
    text: >-
      Each role has a turn cap per step in its role file or config, with a default
      for all roles. When a step reaches its cap, the runner ends it the same way as
      a missing handoff: the work so far is committed or quarantined, the report
      says "turn cap reached", and the step counts as `failed` without a round.
      Turns per step are recorded in the ledger.
  - id: AC3
    text: >-
      The dashboard shows turns per step (latest and average per role) next to cost
      and tokens. The Manager's checkpoint data includes cap hits per role, and the
      Manager raises repeated cap hits as friction with a proposal (raise the cap,
      split the step, or change the brief).
  - id: AC4
    text: >-
      The team measures why the Unit Tester's context is large, using the ledger and
      the step transcripts: whole-file reads, full test-suite output, repeated reads
      of the same files, and so on. It records the findings, with numbers, in an ADR,
      and fixes the top causes. For example: targeted test runs with quiet output
      (`-q`, `-x`, `--no-header`, failures only); read limits and offsets in the
      role rules; per-story design files instead of the 1600-line architecture.md.
      A re-measure on a comparable step shows the average re-sent context per turn
      dropping (target: under 60k tokens for the Unit Tester).
  - id: AC5
    text: >-
      `reference/protocol.md` (PO sessions, turn caps, ledger), SKILL.md, the role
      files and the snapshot fixture describe the changes.
  - id: AC6
    text: >-
      Tests cover the PO boundary and its handoff file, carrying over open questions,
      the turn cap ending a step, turns in the ledger and the snapshot, and the
      Manager's cap-hit data. Coverage stays at or above 95%, and ruff check and ruff
      format are clean.

## User story

As the human paying for the run, I want each role to work from a small, fresh
context, so that cost tracks the work done instead of growing with every turn.
