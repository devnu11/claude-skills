**User story.** As the PO, I want each done story to still match the requirements it traces.
When a later design changes one, the team should check the old story's behaviour instead of
assuming it still holds.

**What happened (2026-10-23, sprint 52).** s179's design changed REQ-160 and added three
requirements. The design gate reported "architect changed requirements that done stories
trace (REQ-160 changed … ): s159" (m353). s180's design changed REQ-164 and REQ-169, which
s159 and s163 trace, and the PO asked for one follow-up covering both (m354). Its note:
"the two stories reserved the same ids … The follow-up should cite whichever ids survive
the renumbering."

What is on origin/main (ab91c1f2):
- s179 landed, renumbered from REQ-190..192 to REQ-193..195 (e362098). Its REQ-160 change
  is commit 2b6f3d79. REQ-160 (`covers: s159/AC10.1-10.3`) now says "`start --resume`
  removes only the worktrees REQ-194 lists, and keeps every open sprint story's (REQ-193)
  and every one whose story has an interrupted step". The old text was "prunes stale
  worktrees, except one whose story has an interrupted step".
- REQ-193: a resume keeps every open sprint story's worktree, ignored files included.
  REQ-194: a resume removes only finished, out-of-sprint and unknown worktrees, plus unpinned
  scratch dirs, one event each. REQ-195: a drifted kept worktree is reported at the next
  step, not removed.
- REQ-190..192 on main are s178's (requirement ids reserved per story), not s179's or
  s180's.
- s180 was reverted to the Product Backlog (m364). Its REQ-164 and REQ-169 changes and its
  REQ-200..202 are not on main: `git diff 0e1e0991 origin/main` touches only REQ-160 and
  adds REQ-190..198. So s163 is out of this story: no requirement it traces changed on
  main.

**Why (fact for the ids and texts, read with `git show origin/main:agile-team/docs/requirements/…`;
certain that s180's next design will raise the s159/s163 trace again).** s159's AC10.2
(`.team/stories/s159.md:271-274`) still says "Stale worktrees left by a crash are pruned on
`start --resume`, except … an interrupted step". That is the rule REQ-160 no longer states.
When s180 is redesigned, its gate will report REQ-164/REQ-169 against s159 and s163 again.
That check belongs to s180's own follow-up then, not this one.

**On acceptance:** the PO amends s159 AC10.2 to REQ-160's wording, or the story records why it differs; no done story's AC should still say a resume prunes every stale worktree.

**Placement:** Product Backlog, not a sprint (m353, m354). Files: none expected in the
runner unless a check fails. `.team/stories/s159.md` (AC10.2) and `reference/protocol.md`'s
resume section are where a mismatch would show. Depends on nothing. Small.
