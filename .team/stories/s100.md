# s100 (A6c): a story timeline, like TeamCity's

**Delivery:** dashboard

Plan: `.team/run/liaison/plans/tool-flow.md` (human-approved 2026-10-13, about 02:15). Its sections "The names of the tools are data" and "Readability" are context for the Architect. Each AC's [tag] is its acceptance environment.

**Where it goes:** in the **Backlog tab's expanded story** (s83), as a "Timeline" section beside the story file, state, events and spend. That's already the one place that shows everything about a story, and it works for done stories. The Board's cards link to it. A **Sprint** setting shows every story in the sprint on one axis.

## User story

As the human, I want a story's whole life on one time axis (every step, every bounce, the runner's stages, the PO's turns, and the waits), so I can see where a story's calendar time went.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1 [dashboard]. Rows.** An expanded story shows a timeline from `story-opened` to done, or to now, with:
  - one row per role that worked on it, in pipeline order;
  - a `PO` row for the PO turns that touched it;
  - a `runner` row for its runner stages (A6b, s99), before-stages and after-stages alike.

  A scope toggle (Story, Sprint), as on the Flow chart, shows every story in a sprint as groups of rows.
- **AC2 [dashboard]. Bars.** Each agent step is a bar in its role's row, coloured by outcome: passed, bounced or disputed. A bounce draws a thin arrow to the row it went back to. Manager rulings are marked.
- **AC3 [dashboard]. Waits.** The gaps are explained. Time waiting on another story, on a question to the human, on a hold, or with the machine asleep is shaded and labelled. The header totals each kind: work, runner, PO, waiting and asleep.
- **AC4 [dashboard]. Live.** While the story runs, the timeline grows from the snapshot stream:
  - a "now" line moves;
  - the running bar extends;
  - the view follows "now" unless the human has scrolled.

  The time axis can be zoomed and panned.
- **AC5 [dashboard]. Tests pin,** under the node harness against fixture events:
  - the rows and their order;
  - a bounce arrow;
  - each wait kind;
  - the header totals;
  - the Sprint scope;
  - the live "now" line.
- **AC-R [dashboard]. Readability.** At 100% browser zoom, every piece of text in the new views is at least **12 CSS px as rendered**: a font size times the scale Mermaid or the SVG applies. That includes the two-line labels, the Flow chart nodes and the axis. Subtle text has a contrast ratio of at least **4.5:1** against its background, in light and dark themes.
  - **Measured:** a node-harness test computes each text element's rendered size and contrast, and fails below the limits.
  - **The Flow chart:** adding nodes must not shrink it below the limit. When it doesn't fit, it scrolls sideways instead of scaling down.
