**User story.** As the human, I want story branches integrated one at a time in an order the PO chooses, smallest first, with overlapping stories kept apart, and parallel steps that never halt or stall the run, so the first sprints with several stories in parallel are safe to run.

**The human's direction (2026-10-19), in their words:**
> "when it comes to integrating the story branches, there is no parallelism. Each story can go to its own story branch on GH, but then the PO needs to order their integration. Prioritize the small stories first, since the team who did the larger story will have more context to lean on in resolving any merge conflicts. PO also needs to be careful about scheduling stories with conflicting work. It could review the architecture spec and look for overlap before handing off further."

That direction is AC1 and AC2.

**The review findings this fixes:** Fable's code review of sprint 49 is `.team/run/liaison/reports/2026-10-18-sprint49-code-review.md`.
- **B2 → AC3.** `hold_ci` leaves `Landed(merge, pushed=merge)`. `reorder_queue` puts the story behind the next one, and that story lands. The held story's resume path then pushes a stale merge: the lease is refused, `follow_remote` sees no remote move, and `halt_diverged` halts the run (`landing.py:168-170, 397-406, 526-529`).
- **B1 → AC4.** `_switch_refusal` runs before the START sync, the stack is pruned only at the END sync, and `count_ahead` counts rebased commits as ahead (`po_tools.py:357-366`, `switchover.py:61-74`). The liaison cleared the sprint-49 entry and moved the team branch by hand once (2026-10-18).
- **M1 → AC5.** `run_land` holds `main_lock` through `wait_ci` (up to `ci_timeout_s`), while the resumed path moves the checkout without it (`landing.py:168-175, 379-386, 415-435`).
- **M2 → AC6.** `run_boundary` runs `boundaries.step()` inline (`scheduler.py:448-456`).
- **M3 → AC7.** The PO's `run_role` calls `runtime.run_role` directly (`po_tools.py:284-293` against `scheduler.py:366-369`).
- **M4 → AC8.** `push_sprint` and `follow_remote` bookkeeping (`landing.py:474-481, 538-542`, `dispatch.py:1347-1353`).

The review's 9 MINOR findings aren't in scope. Today's live state already passes the one-time switch (the liaison cleared it by hand), so AC4 fixes the next time.

**Placement:** the human (2026-10-19), option 1: this story alone in the next sprint, the first story-branches sprint. With one story, the parallel-landing fault in AC3 can't happen yet. After it, s161 and the tiny unattended story run together as the first parallel sprint.
