title: Session limit and crashes stop the runner cleanly with a saved status and a note
delivery: cli
acceptance:
  - id: AC1
    text: >-
      When the SDK raises a session or usage limit error (e.g. claude_agent_sdk
      ResultError "You've hit your session limit · resets 2:20pm (America/Edmonton)"),
      either in the PO's own query or inside a role step started by `run_role`, the
      runner does not exit with a traceback. It saves run status `blocked` with a
      reason that keeps the reset text (e.g. "session limit, resets 2:20pm
      (America/Edmonton)"), posts a `blocked` note with that text to
      `.team/run/outbox.jsonl`, prints one plain line saying what happened and what to
      do (`start --resume` after the reset), and exits with a documented exit code.
  - id: AC2
    text: >-
      Any other unexpected exception from the PO loop, and SIGTERM (`stop --now`), is
      also handled at the top level. The run status leaves `running` (a non-running
      status the architect picks, e.g. `failed`), a note naming the error is posted for
      the liaison, the full traceback goes to a log file under `.team/run/`, and the
      process exits non-zero. No raw traceback is printed as the only output.
  - id: AC3
    text: >-
      Limit detection is data, not branching: a table maps the error kind or message
      pattern to the stored reason. The reset text is taken from the error message, not
      computed.
  - id: AC4
    text: >-
      `start --resume` after a limit or crash stop clears the stored blocked or failed
      status and continues the PO session as it does today. A `blocked` status saved for
      a limit does not leave the resumed PO refused on every `run_role`.
  - id: AC5
    text: >-
      `agile-team status` shows the stored reason (e.g. "session limit, resets 2:20pm
      (America/Edmonton)") next to the run status.
  - id: AC6
    text: >-
      `agile-team/SKILL.md` (liaison: what to do on a limit or crash note),
      `reference/protocol.md` (run statuses, exit codes, log file) and the README row
      are updated in this story.
  - id: AC7
    text: >-
      Tests use a fake `query_fn` that raises the limit error, or another error,
      mid-stream, both in the PO session and in a role step. Coverage stays at or above
      95%, and ruff check and ruff format are clean.

## User story

As the human running the team on my Claude login, I want the runner to stop
cleanly when I hit my session limit or it crashes, so that `status` and the
liaison tell me what happened and when I can resume, and I don't have to dig
through a traceback.

## Notes

Observed in the last run: the runner died with a `claude_agent_sdk`
`ResultError` "You've hit your session limit · resets 2:20pm
(America/Edmonton)" and left only a traceback.

Human's decision (m3): stop cleanly only. No auto-sleep or auto-resume until
the reset time.

Related: s9 (what an interrupted step leaves behind) and s10 (cost lost on a
crash). This story owns the top-level handling. s9 and s10 build on it, so
this story runs first.

Relevant code: `cli._run`, `po_tools.PoRun.run`, `dispatch.Runtime._execute`,
`state.RunStatus`, `relay.Relay.post`.
