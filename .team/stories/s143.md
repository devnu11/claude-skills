## User story

As the architect, I want requirements to trace story ACs whose ids contain dots, so the design gate sees s141's ACs (AC1.1 to AC8.3) as traced.

## Origin

Found by the architect in the s141 redesign: the current pattern `(s\d+)/([A-Za-z][\w-]*)` rejects `s141/AC1.1`, so no requirement can trace s141's ACs and the design gate bounces. The architect proposes the pattern `[\w.-]*` for the AC part. Opened by the PO as a separate small story, ahead of s141 (listed in its frontmatter).
