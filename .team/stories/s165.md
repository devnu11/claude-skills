title: "Bug: a run whose story step was cut off can't resume, because the cut-off step's record is built with its fields out of order"
acceptance:
  - id: AC1
    environment: cli
    text: >-
      When a run stops while a story step runs in its worktree (a crash, a kill, a reboot),
      `agile-team start --resume` starts. The cut-off step ends as `stopped`, the story keeps
      its worktree with any uncommitted edits and the step's session, and the story's
      `interrupted` record in `state.json` names that step's role, the story, the step's brief
      and the stop's cause.
  - id: AC2
    environment: cli
    text: >-
      The next step on the story is the same role's, at the step the story was on when it was
      cut off. Its brief says that role's step was cut off, names the cause, and says the
      uncommitted edits are still in the worktree; the step runs in that worktree with those
      edits in place and with the role's kept session.
---

**User story.** As the human, I want a run to come back after the machine reboots in the
middle of a story step, so a reboot costs one step, not a stopped team.

**What happened (2026-10-19, sprint 50).** The machine rebooted at about 18:48 while s163's
developer was at `implement` in its worktree. Every `agile-team start --resume` then failed
with `ValueError: '<the step's brief>' is not a valid Cause`, and the unattended job
ended `failed`. The team stayed stopped until the liaison hotfixed the pinned runner, with
the human's approval.

**Cause (fact, read from the code).** `Runtime._resume_story` (`dispatch.py:1130`) builds
`Interruption(cause, flying.role, story.id, flying.brief, None)`. `Interruption`'s fields
are `role, story, brief, cause, sha` (`salvage.py:84-95`), and its `__post_init__` turns
`cause` into a `Cause`, so the brief lands in `cause` and the constructor raises. The
storyless path builds it in the right order (`salvage.py:120`). The bug came in with s159's
I2 (commit `571374f1`, a fixup of s159). The s159 design's I2 (`docs/design/s159.md:590`)
writes the call in the same wrong order; the code followed it. The design text moves with
the fix. Nothing in `tests/` drives `Runtime.recover_stories` with a story in flight, which
is how the bug passed CI.

**Known limits, not fixed here.** The kept session carries no `reported` cost, so the
resumed step may be billed the cut-off step's spend again (K4). `_has_ended` returns true
when the log has no start or end for the role on the story, so a missing `events.jsonl`
would skip the `stopped` step-end.

**The live hotfix.** With the human's approval (2026-10-19, "do 1"), the liaison swapped the
arguments in `.team/run/runner-pin/agile_team/dispatch.py:1130` only. The repo still has
the bug, so the next re-pin would bring it back unless this story has landed.

with s161, s162 and s164, as a bug fix, and before any re-pin. It shares `dispatch.py` with
s161 (line 491) and s164 (line 1319) and `docs/design/s159.md` with s161, in different
