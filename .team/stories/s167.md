**User story.** As the team, I want the suite's checks to take the same time on a busy
machine as on an idle one, so parallel story gates don't fail on a timeout that has nothing
to do with their stories.

**What happened (2026-10-20, sprint 51).** With five stories in parallel, the PO reported
(m293) that `test_s104_flow_page::test_the_flow_is_not_shrunk_to_the_container` timed out
past its 1 s limit and blocked s164 at quality. On an idle machine at d3518fbe it takes
0.17–0.18 s; the load average reached 41 that morning. Whether the same timeout is behind
the red CI on s162 and s166 (m293) is not known; this story fixes the checks, not CI.

**Cause (fact, profiled 2026-10-20; see the review).** The check runs
`re.findall(r"[^{}]*(?:#flow|\.fc)[^{}]*\{[^}]*\}", text)` over the whole page
(`tests/test_s104_flow_page.py:21`). The leading `[^{}]*` may start at every position of a
brace-free stretch, and each start that finds no `#flow` or `.fc` before the next brace
scans to the stretch's end, so the cost grows with the square of each stretch's length. The
3.9 KB of body HTML between `</style>` and `<script` is one such stretch (0.06 s alone), and
the script's ~3,500 short stretches add 0.1 s. At 0.17 s the check has only a 6x margin
under the 1 s unit limit, which a load average of 41 removes; no sprint-51 branch changes
the page. `test_s127_page.rule()` has the same shape (`([^{}]+)\{([^{}]*)\}`,
`tests/test_s127_page.py:12`, 0.048 s per call). Anchoring the match at a brace or the
start (for example `(?<![^{}])`) gives the same matches in 0.003 s; design decides.

**Placement:** the current sprint (sprint 51), because it blocks s164's quality gate. It
touches only `tests/test_s104_flow_page.py` and `tests/test_s127_page.py`; no sprint-51
branch touches those files or the dashboard page. The PO asked for `suite_runs: 3` (m293);
it is left out because three idle runs pass today without a fix and can't reproduce the
load, which is why AC1 and AC3 bound the checks' time instead.
