# s96: Time per role on the Flow chart, and a Time measure in the "Tokens by" chart

**Delivery:** dashboard

## User story

As the human, I want to see how long each role and story takes, next to what it costs, so I can see where the time goes without asking for a report.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1. Step durations in the snapshot.** Each finished agent step's wall time (step-start to step-end, from events.jsonl) is in the snapshot with its role, story, model and sprint. No new event kind is needed. A running step carries its start time.
- **AC2. Flow boxes show time.** Every agent box shows the role's total time for the Flow chart's chosen scope (Story, Sprint or Run), next to its tokens and dollars. The active box also shows how long its running step has been going, updating live.
- **AC3. A Time measure in "Tokens by".** The chart's measure toggle (Tokens, Dollars) gains a third button, Time. It's one row in the `UNITS` table, not a branch. With Time chosen:
  - the title reads "Time by";
  - the bars are the total step time per group (Role, Story or Model, as the existing group toggle chooses), labelled in h and min;
  - each bar's tooltip gives the step count and its share of the total time.
- **AC4. What time counts.** Only agent steps are counted. The runner's own stages between steps (gate runs, syncs, presentation builds) aren't, and the chart says so in its legend ("agent steps only; runner stages: see A6").
- **AC5. Tests pin:**
  - durations summed per role, story and model from fixture events;
  - the scope toggle on the box times;
  - a running step's elapsed time;
  - the Time button and its title, under the node harness (`tests/dashboard_node.py`).

  The snapshot fixture is updated.
