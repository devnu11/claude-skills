  - id: AC3
    text: >-
      To the liaison. The post: a post to the liaison is appended to a liaison inbox
      in the run directory, tagged with the story id. Waking the liaison: `agile-team
      watch` gets a new trigger, `liaison_message`, so the liaison is woken at once,
      not at its heartbeat. Replying: the liaison replies with a CLI command (for
      example `agile-team reply <story> <text>`), and the reply appears in the thread.
  - id: AC4
    text: >-
      Event and order. Every post and reply is an event on the story, with author,

      trigger, and the localhost rule.

## User story

As the human, I want to discuss a story from its Backlog tab entry, with the PO
or with the liaison, and see the replies there, so feedback and discussion stay
with the story.
