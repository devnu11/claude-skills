      step (role, story, step), the time of the last turn, the current tool with its
      start time, and the tokens and dollars of the step so far. It keeps beating while a

## User story

As the human watching the run, I want the dashboard to show that tokens are being spent,
at what rate and on what, and to raise a loud signal when that stops when it should not
or happens when it should not, so I can see at a glance whether the team is working or
something is wrong. The signal comes from the runner, not from the agent.
