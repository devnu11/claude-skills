# The Flow chart shows the logical flow, readable at real size, built in HTML

## User story

As the human, I want the Flow chart to show the logical flow of a story, with
every role's numbers and tools, readable at 100% zoom, and a dot that shows work
moving, so I can see at a glance who's working and where time and money go.

## The mock is the spec

The layout is mock **variant N**. For anything the ACs leave open, the mock is
the spec: `.team/run/liaison/flow-chart-mock-approved.html` (open it in a browser
with `#n`; functions `variantN`, `nBox`, `nTool`, `afterK`). The architect and the
developer should read it; the customer proxy should compare the real tab with
it. Its data is made up except the role and tool totals. (The ACs as the human
wrote them name `plans/flow-chart-mock.html`; the approved copy above is the one
to use.)
