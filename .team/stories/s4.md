      role, story and model, plus cumulative over time. `questions` holds open relay

      `requirements` holds the list with status, the AC coverage matrix and the change

## User story

As the human, I want a read-only dashboard server, reachable from other
machines on the lab network, that turns the run's files into one live snapshot
and pushes updates as they happen, so I can watch the team without reading
JSON files.
