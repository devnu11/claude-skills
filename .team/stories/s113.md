## Why

- `po-turn` events record the PO's `model_s`, `tool_s` and `waiting_s` (A6a), and the Time chart counts them.
- The Flow chart's PO box (`flowGroupLines`) shows only tokens, cost and a share bar in Sprint and Run scope, and "—" in Story scope. `po-turn` events carry no story. s102 AC2 covered the Time chart, not the Flow box.

## User story

As the human, I want the PO's box on the Flow chart to show its time like every
other role's box, so I can see what the PO costs in time.
