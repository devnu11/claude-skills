acceptance:
  - id: AC1
    text: >-
      When a story is blocked on a human decision, the runner parks it, all in code.
      It moves the story's commits onto a story branch, takes them out of the team
      branch, holds the story with a reason that names the open question, and routes
      the next ready story. The question waits in the relay for the human.
  - id: AC2
    text: >-
      When the answer arrives, the story's branch is brought back (rebased or merged
      onto the team branch, following the human's rebase rules in s29) and the story
      resumes at the step where it stopped, with the answer in its brief.
  - id: AC3
    text: >-
      Hashes stay valid. Parking must not leave `state.json` (story `commit`, review
      and design files) pointing at commits that are no longer on the team branch.
      The architect chooses the method (for example a story branch the team branch
      merges back, never a rewrite of the team branch) and records how the recorded
      hashes are kept reachable.
  - id: AC4
    text: >-
      Parking is the default for a human question that blocks only one story. A
      question that blocks every story still stops the run as today. Parking is
      logged as an event (story, branch, question id) and appears in the liaison note.
  - id: AC5
    text: >-
      The dashboard shows parked stories, their branches and the open questions they
      wait on. The snapshot fixture is updated.
  - id: AC6
    text: >-
      `reference/protocol.md`, SKILL.md and the PO role file describe parking and
      unparking. Tests cover park, carry on with another story, unpark with the answer,
      hash validity, a question that blocks every story, and a conflict on unpark
      (which goes to the PO as a hand-over). Coverage stays at or above 95%, and ruff
      check and ruff format are clean.

## User story

As the human, I want a story that waits on me to be set aside on its own branch
so the team keeps working on another, and to pick up where it stopped when I
answer, so that nothing idles overnight.

## Notes

The human: the story should "branch off the code for that story, halt work, and
resume another story." And: "Honestly that might be a useful addition to the
skill." Depends on s29 (rebase rules, branches) and s19 (routing in code).
