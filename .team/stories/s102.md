# s102 (A6e): runner stages on the Flow chart and in the Time chart

**Delivery:** dashboard

Plan: `.team/run/liaison/plans/tool-flow.md` (human-approved 2026-10-13, about 02:15). Its sections "The names of the tools are data" and "Readability" are context for the Architect. Each AC's [tag] is its acceptance environment.

## User story

As the human, I want the runner's stages drawn as stages of their own on the Flow chart and counted in the Time chart, so the gate and boundary time stops being invisible.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1 [dashboard]. Flow chart nodes.** Each runner stage group is its own node, where it runs:
  - the environment preparation before acceptance;
  - the gate after implement;
  - the e2e gate;
  - the boundary.

  A node shows its last and total time for the Flow chart's scope (Story, Sprint or Run), and lights up while it runs.
- **AC2 [dashboard]. The Time chart.** The "Time by" measure (t35, now s96) counts runner stages as a `runner` row when grouped by role, and the PO's model time and `waiting_s` as separate segments. The legend no longer says "agent steps only".
- **AC3 [dashboard]. Model and tool share.** Each agent box on the Flow chart shows a model and tool share bar for the scope.
- **AC4 [dashboard]. Tests pin:** the node per stage group and its position, the runner row and PO segments, and the share bar's numbers, all against fixture events.
- **AC-R [dashboard]. Readability.** At 100% browser zoom, every piece of text in the new views is at least **12 CSS px as rendered**: a font size times the scale Mermaid or the SVG applies. That includes the two-line labels, the Flow chart nodes and the axis. Subtle text has a contrast ratio of at least **4.5:1** against its background, in light and dark themes.
  - **Measured:** a node-harness test computes each text element's rendered size and contrast, and fails below the limits.
  - **The Flow chart:** adding nodes must not shrink it below the limit. When it doesn't fit, it scrolls sideways instead of scaling down.
