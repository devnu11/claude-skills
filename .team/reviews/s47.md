## W rules

- W1: no code change, as the design's log analysis supports. The driver test
  `test_an_answer_wakes_the_run_when_no_po_session_runs` covers the path with
  no PO session: the PO asks and ends its turn, the fake sleep writes the
  answer, the second PO prompt carries `The human answered m1 …`, and a
  `human_message` hand-over is logged. AC1 and AC2 are met.
- W3/W4: `mark()` keeps the status, and a halt or graceful idle replaces the
  reason. Other readers of `status_reason` (`cli.status_report`,
  `dispatch._halt_text`) are unaffected. `_halt_text` applies only to halted
  statuses, and those never carry a waiting reason.
- W5: the stderr line and the stored reason are unchanged, as required.
- W6: the stall lines are unchanged. That is correct, because `_idle` never
  stalls while a question is open.
