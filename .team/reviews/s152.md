## Findings

- **F1 (s152/AC1.1, AC3.1; REQ-088, REQ-091), test gap, blocking, unit-tester. Still
  open (fourth round).** Add these tests in `tests/test_s152_start.py`, named
  `test_ac1_1_*`. Use `tests/e2e/test_s59_rebase_e2e.make_world` or the scratch-repo helpers
  in `test_upstream.py`, and do not stub `behind` or `rebase_onto`:
  (a) Main has moved by one commit, and a story's `commit` is a team commit. After one start
  `sync` step, HEAD must be on `origin/main` (`merge-base --is-ancestor`) and the story's
  `commit` must be the rebased copy. There must be a `rebased` event with `rewritten >= 1`,
  `sync_from` must be empty, and the stage must be `check`.
  (b) Main has not moved. HEAD keeps its sha, and a `current` event carries `START_CURRENT`.
  (c) The usual start: the previous sprint's commits are already on main as rebase-merged
  copies. HEAD must end on `origin/main` with no conflict and no hold.
  The other way to close F1 is for DevOps to correct the `## Tooling` record so it says
  stubs. That is not enough on its own, though: nothing would show that the pinned
  `sync_from` path works against a real repository.
- **F2, F3, F4, F8: closed.** F8 was fixed in 488f2d71.
- **F5 (s152/AC2.3; REQ-090), cosmetic, still open.** `KNOWN_LINE` shows the full sha, while
  the liaison's note shows 12 characters.
- **F6 (s152/AC2.2; REQ-089), open question for the liaison, still open.** The AC says the
  ids are recorded "in the `sprint-start` event". Design S6 / ADR-0077 §6 puts them on the
  start boundary's closing `done` event instead. The AC text has not been amended.
- **F7 (no AC), cosmetic, integration-tester, still open.** The docstring of
  `tests/e2e/test_s108_start_e2e.py` still says the start sync "changes nothing".
