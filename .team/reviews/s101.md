## Copy/paste search

- **m2:** `groupNests` and `groupLines` both do `group.bars.filter(b => b.key …).sort((a, b) => a.from - b.from)`. A small `keyedBars(group)` helper would remove the repeat.
- **m7:** `callSvg` repeats the `tlSeen` / `tlClamp(x1, x2)` preamble of `barSvg` and `sleepSvg`, making three copies. A `span(plot, from, to)` helper could return `{x1, x2}` or null.
- The `-sleep` and `-unfinished` pattern strings share their attributes. They are short and literal, so this is acceptable.

## Minor findings (don't block)

- **m1:** `tests/test_server.py::test_routes_are_exactly_four` now asserts five routes; the name should be updated.
- **m3:** `MISC_FAILED` is cached in `ui.miscCalls`, so a transient fetch failure can't be retried without reloading the page. This conforms to N10 ("when undefined"). Consider clearing the key on close.
- **m4:** A running step's misc list is fetched once and never refreshed while the step keeps gathering calls. This is close to the known limit "the misc list can drift"; the customer doc could mention it.
- **m5:** In the legend, `unfinished` and `waiting` share the `--axis` swatch, so the hatch isn't visible there. This conforms to the design ("drawn as its fill").
- **m6:** The parent label's pointer cursor comes from the CSS rule `.tl [data-tl-keys]` instead of N13's inline `style`. The effect is the same.
- **Docs:** `reference/protocol.md`, `docs/customer/dashboard.md` and the `architecture.md` rows are not in this diff. The design gives them to the architect at ship (charter: docs move with behaviour in the same story).
