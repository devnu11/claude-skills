   - **AC2**: the `panelState` stage fixture with an older `boundary` event (`reason: "check: done"`). Check that `[who, when, line]` contains `gate.tests+coverage` and the description, and contains neither the event's `rowOf` `what` nor its first body line. Also: no `description` gives `line === ""`. This AC was the human's headline bug, and nothing executes it now.
   - **AC4a pin**: `manager_due` holding the goal, then exactly one flag whose title has the reason, and no pill's visible text holds the goal. With `[]`, no flag. The s112 tests cover the flag but not "no pill holds the sprint goal".
   - **AC4b pin**: `renderLivePanel` with `#lp-who`, `#lp-when` regex, `#lp-line` and `#lp-msg`, plus clip of 8 lines keeping 4 and `-webkit-line-clamp: 3`.

   t70 (test first, fails on today's code) cannot be shown for AC1 and AC2 without these tests. Grepping `tests/` finds no call to `sprintGoal`, `stageLine` or `stagePanel` with a boundary event.
2. **Circular assertion** in `test_s112_activity_page_e2e.py::test_goal_is_the_task_text_and_its_hover_title`: `expected = goalText(view)` checks `renderGoal` against the function it calls. 3. Minor: the tests in `test_s119_header_gaps.py` have no docstrings, unlike the s112 files (house rule: brief docstrings).
