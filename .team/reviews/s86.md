(`waiting_on: []`). This matches the design contract.

## Copy/paste

There is no repeated block. `_dependency_keys` repeats
`_priority(files).queue(stories.values())` from `_position`, as the design asked, but both
recompute the queue for every story (O(n²) per snapshot). It is fine at this backlog size.
A shared `_queue(files)` helper would make it DRY. This is not blocking.
