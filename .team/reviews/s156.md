  `movable_sprint_backlog`, `stuck_lines`, `stuck_blocked_text`, `Router._stuck`,

  `_sprint_end`, although the design keeps it. A developer edit to
  `docs/design/s156.md` was quarantined and the quarantine then reverted, but the
  design still lists the old names. The architect should bring the design in line
  so the next reader isn't misled.

  `test_ac1_a_held_boundary_or_empty_sprint_stays_the_quiet_stall` tests only the

  Sprint Backlog stories (these should give `stalled`, not `sprint_backlog_stuck`).

  add that case or rename the test.
