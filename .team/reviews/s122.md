## Findings

**F1: missing test (design checklist item 11). Owner: integration-tester.**
`tests/e2e/test_s122_activity_e2e.py` does not exist. The only coverage is the
SAMPLES row added to `test_s112_activity_page_e2e.py`, which checks that the row renders
and that its body goes through markdown. It does not check "stories added", "sprint 1",
the reason, or the story ids in `Stories: …`. A typo in `what`/`to` or a wrong
`e.data.stories` key would pass. AC4 ("It's visible") is the user-facing promise here.

**F2: stray formatting edit in `index.html`. Owner: developer-cli.**
`const ACT_TO = {` became `const ACT_TO ={`. This is unrelated to the story and not
caught by ruff (HTML). Revert that line.
