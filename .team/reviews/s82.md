# Review s82: the service stop waits for its whole group and its port (round 1)

**Verdict: ship.** No defects. Diff reviewed: `17efde9..b6a2d43` (sandbox.py,
presentation.py, tests/test_s82.py, protocol.md, architecture.md). Coverage

## Cause (AC1)

The design names a real bug: `stop()` waited only for the `sh -c` leader. Under
dash the leader dies at SIGTERM before `uv`/python close the socket. The
reasoning holds, and the fix does not depend on the shell. Only Linux CI can
prove it (AC4). The e2e step comes after design-review (`e2e` in
`[gates].steps`), and the integration-tester still owes
`tests/e2e/test_service_stop_e2e.py`. It does not exist yet, which is expected at
this step.

## Nits (non-blocking)

1. The names `end_group` (a live `Popen`) and `_end_group` (a recorded stale pid)
   differ only by an underscore. A rename would help a later reader
   (`_end_recorded`?).
2. `_end_group` (the stale path) still does not wait after SIGKILL. This was
   there before s82, and the next start's `_refuse_busy` holds anyway.
3. `_group_alive` calls `os.killpg` directly, not through the `system` seam.
   That is fine while `sandbox` is in LEGACY, but it needs a `system` wrapper
   when sandbox is converted (s76/s77).
4. In a container where pid 1 does not reap, orphaned zombies keep the group
   "alive". The stop then takes 15 s but still returns `None`, because the port
   is free. GitHub's ubuntu runners are not affected.
5. `Prepared._end` carries `# type: ignore[arg-type]`. Passing `self.process`
   in as an argument would avoid the ignore, but this is cosmetic.
