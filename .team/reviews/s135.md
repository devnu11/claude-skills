### F4: the F3 fix has no test (s135/AC1, AC6; REQ-037). Owner: unit-tester

The round-0 F3 asked for a test next to
`test_an_unreadable_file_gives_its_line_plus_the_option_lines` that uses
`amend`. No such test was added; `target_status_problems` turns up in no
test. The behaviour to pin:

- `story amend s999 missing.md --approved-by none` prints both `cannot read …` and `no story 's999'`, and queues nothing;
- the same with a finished target prints the `FINISHED_TARGET` line;
- with a readable draft, the unknown-target line appears **once**. Today
  `_target_problems` and `target_status_problems` share `_status_problems`
  and only one of them runs. A test should guard against a regression that
  prints the line twice.

The lines run, so coverage stays at 100%. But nothing asserts the output,
so a regression would pass.
