## Test coverage note (s137/AC3, REQ-030, REQ-031)

The unit tests in this step only check the page source. The behaviour cases
AC3 asks for are assigned in the story's Tooling section and the design's
checklist to `tests/e2e/test_s137_live_numbers_e2e.py`, which the
integration-tester writes at the e2e step. That file does not exist yet.
Those cases are: non-zero numbers on a first step in Story scope, growth
between snapshots, no double count in the settling window or after the step,
an earlier ledger row, an idle role unchanged, a step outside the scope, and
the PO. **The e2e step must write all of the design checklist's cases.**
Without them AC3 is not met.
