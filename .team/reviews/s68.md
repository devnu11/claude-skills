## Corner cases checked

- **`&&` chain in this repo's `coverage` command.** First suite short, no failures:
  it stops, the output's last TOTAL is the short one, and the shortfall bounce is
  correct. First suite at 100% and second failing: `tests_failed` → `tests fail`.
  First at 100% and second short: `parse_coverage` takes the *last* TOTAL line, so
  the shortfall bounce reports the second suite's figure. All correct.
- pytest-cov's `FAIL Required … Total coverage: 80.00%` line holds "TOTAL", but
  `found[-1]` takes the 80.00, as it did before.
- Collection errors (`1 error in …`) → `tests fail`. Correct.
- The accepted baseline consequence (design § Known consequence) is pinned by
  `test_baseline_does_not_let_a_nonzero_exit_pass`.
