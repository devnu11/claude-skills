### D1 (major, fixed, untested): a landing revert and a land ran at once. s163/AC1, REQ-167

The fix is correct. `begin_revert` runs in `start_revert` before the task exists, so on the next
tick `next_up()` → `_is_live(state.landing)` is true while the revert runs, and `check_land` gets
the reverted story, not a waiting one. A restart drops `reverting` (it lives in memory) and the
revert queue (also in memory), so Q5's stale-landing clear still fires after a restart, as the
design intends.

**Still needed:** the §7 test "A revert and a land never run at once": a queued revert of a landed
story and a story at `land`, the revert started, then a tick while the revert task is blocked. The
land must not start, and `state.landing` must still be the reverted story. No test references
`begin_revert`, `reverting` or `REVERT_KEY` with a land (a grep of `tests/` finds none).

### D4 (minor, new): a revert that raises keeps the landing slot until restart. s163/AC1, REQ-167 (design Q5)

`run_revert` calls `self.landings.finish(story.id)` only after `land_revert` returns. If
`land_revert` raises (a `GitError` escaping, a cancelled task at `stop_tasks`), `finish` never
runs. Before this fix the stale `state.landing` was cleared on the next tick, because the story was
not live. Now `reverting` keeps it live, so **every landing waits until the runner restarts**. That
is the stall AC1 and the story title say must not happen. `Scheduler.land` has the same shape
(`begin` … `finish` with no `finally`). There, a story that stays at `land` is the resume, so it
does no harm. For the revert, put `finish` in a `finally` (or clear `reverting` when the
`REVERT_KEY` task is reaped), and test a raising `land_revert` followed by a land that starts.

### D2 (minor, open from round 0): `find_merged` crashes with no upstream. s163/AC4, REQ-170 (design S2)

Unchanged. `po_tools.py:375` still passes `rt.upstream` to `switchover.find_merged` with no
`rt.upstream is None` guard. `start_sprint` raises `AttributeError` when there is no upstream and
the stack has an entry with a `head`. S2: "With no upstream … nothing is dropped." No test.

### D3 (minor, open from round 0): a failed `git diff` becomes "changed files". s163/AC2, REQ-168 (design F3)

Unchanged. `overlaps.py:101` still splits `git diff --name-only`'s output without checking its

reads `git show :<design>` (the main checkout's index) when `other.branch` is empty. Fix it, or say
in the design that the index is the intended source.
