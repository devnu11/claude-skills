## Findings

- **F1 (s165/AC1, REQ-174): the design's tests are missing.** The design's "Changes per module"
  and "Test checklist" ask for five new tests in `tests/test_s165_resume_story.py`. None are
  in the file:
  1. a resume through `cli._recover_stories(runtime, Start("", StartMode.RESUME))` with no
     `halt_kind` raises nothing and records `Cause.KILLED`;
  2. the same with `halt_kind = HaltKind.CRASH` records `Cause.CRASH`;
  3. after `recover_stories`, `state.load(rt.config.run_dir)` gives back the story with
     `interrupted == Interruption(ROLE, "s1", BRIEF, cause, None)` and `in_flight is None`
     (the `state.json` round trip, which AC1 names);
  4. `{Cause(k) for k in HaltKind} <= set(Cause)` (R2);
  5. after `cut_off`, `story.step == "implement"` (see F2).

  The CLI path in 1 and 2 is what the human actually ran (`agile-team start --resume`). Today
  nothing tests that `_recover_stories` turns the halt kind into the cause, or that the record
  survives `state.json`. AC1 says "the story's `interrupted` record in `state.json`", and no
  test reads `state.json`.
- **F2 (s165/AC2, REQ-175): "at the step the story was on" is not asserted.** No test checks
  that `story.step` is still `implement` after the resume (design R3, item 5 above). The
  existing next-step test plans a `ROLE` step, but it would pass even if the resume had
  changed the step.
- **F3 (s165/AC1, REQ-174): R2 has no guard.** If someone adds a `HaltKind` without a
  matching `Cause`, every resume after that halt raises `ValueError` in `_recover_stories`,
  which is the same class of failure as this bug. Item 4 above is the one-line guard.
- **F4 (fits no AC; story process): the Tooling section is wrong.** `b24b818e` says AC1 is
  covered by "the design's _recover_stories, state.json round-trip and HaltKind-to-Cause tests"
  and AC2 by "the design's story.step == 'implement' check". None of these exist. Once F1 is
  fixed the section is true. If any test is dropped, the section must say so.
- **F5 (fits no AC; handoffs):** the quality-czar's and the developer's handoffs say the fix
  was "already in dispatch.py with tests passing". That's true of the code, but the design
  says outright that the build *adds* the missing tests, so "nothing to do" was the wrong
  reading.
