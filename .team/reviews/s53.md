## New defects (block)

- **D4. The role file states a wrong release path (developer).** The new line says "A held `needs_manager` story is released by `run_role` of the manager."
  - No manager ruling clears `po_hold`. Only `story_admin.release`, `keep`, `cancel` and `unclear` assign it (grep `po_hold` in `agile_team/`).
  - So after the ruling the story is `ACTIVE` and still held, and `_movable` (router.py:293) keeps skipping it. A PO who follows this text waits forever.
  - The correct text: run the manager first, then `release_story` (which then passes, because the story is ACTIVE with a `po_hold`).

  `fixtures/snapshot.json` has only `po_hold: null` and the `title` keys. The fixture is the page's contract (charter), and checklist item 17 (node harness: the pill, the Cancelled group) can't use it without these cases.
