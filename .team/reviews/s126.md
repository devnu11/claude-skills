## Tests to add or fix (unit-tester)

1. **AC9, node harness** (`tests/test_s126_flow_stop_checks.py`, as the design
   names it, using the existing node `_run` helper pattern from
   `tests/e2e/test_s102_stages_page_e2e.py`):
   - From the fixture, developer-cli's after boxes render
     `stop: suite + coverage · ↻ 2 · … · 1 refused`, and
     `stop: lint fix + check · ↻ 1 · …` with no `refused`.
   - In Story scope on another story, there is no `refused`.
   - A copy of the fixture with an open `stop.suite` row with
     `attempt: {n: 2, of: 3}` and `tests` renders
     `▶ stop: suite + coverage · attempt 2 of 3` and the Pass/Fail bar.
2. **SC13, digest on a real git repo:** the digest is the same before and after
   committing the change, and differs after a file edit and after a new
   untracked file. Replace `test_git_content_digest_exists` with this test.
3. **Config:** a `[toolchain.files]` key in the config overrides the preset's
   key. A `fix` or `lint_files` without `{files}` gives the exact rule message.
   A budget of `0` gives "gates.stop_check_budget_s must be positive".
4. **SC6:** a handoff with status `"odd"` is refused with HANDOFF_BAD and runs
   no command.
5. **Weak assertion:** `test_fixture_has_stop_check_events_and_attempts` line 31
   reads `A or '"attempt": {' in FIXTURE`. The `or` makes the exact check
   pointless. Assert the exact attempt-1 and attempt-2 objects.
6. Nice to have: `coverage_rows` keeps the miss rows and TOTAL and drops the
   0-miss rows. After the cap, the gate bounces as today (the checklist's
   "the gate then runs and bounces").
