## Notes (non-blocking; none needs a round)

- **N1** `git ls-files` C-quotes paths that hold non-ASCII or control characters
  (`core.quotePath`). `pick_tracked` would then miss such a file, and the dispute
  would be rejected with `UNTRACKED`. This fails safe and the message is clear.
  A later fix could use `-z`.
- **N2** A whitespace-only `reason` (`"   "`) passes the "a reason is required"
  check. The design says "empty", so this follows the contract, but
  `.strip()` would be stricter.
- **N3** `dispute_settled` sets `bounce_to="tests"`, and `_retry_here` sets it
  again. It is harmless and redundant.
- **N4** `_pass_tests` has 9 body lines, against the charter's "ideally under 8".
  `pass_tests_refusal` runs the whole suite synchronously inside an async tool.
  That is acceptable for a rare manual tool.
