## Blocking items (carried from round 1)

- **D1. `record_ci_bug` and the bug-first rule are untested (AC4, AC7, CI11, CI14).**
  Tests needed:
  - the three refusals: `NO_CI_HOLD` (once with no boundary, once with a different
    hold), `UNKNOWN_BUG` and `DONE_BUG`;
  - the accepted case: boundary becomes `None`, `upstream.closed == sprint`, the
    report says "checks failed, story sN opened", `ci_bug == sN`, and a `done`
    event carries `ci: red, story: sN`;
  - `start_sprint`: refused with `CI_BUG_FIRST` when the bug is not first;
    accepted when it is first, and `ci_bug` is cleared; a done bug story gives no
    refusal and is cleared.
- **D2. The non-green outcomes of the boundary runner are untested (AC7).**
  `FakePrs` must be able to script a sequence of check lists and a failed log.
  Cases needed:
  - red → hold `ci_failed`, with the failing lines in `checks` and in the
    hand-over, and the sprint stories waiting;
  - timeout → hold `ci_pending` with a reason, then `continue_boundary` goes
    green;
  - red after continue → held again;
  - no checks, or no non-draft PR → `done` with `ci: none` and `CI_NONE_REPORT`;
  - permission `no`, or `gh` missing → `local` event with `ci: none`, and the CI
    stage never runs;
  - a plain start skips `ci` and a restacking start runs it;
  - `UpstreamError` → `boundary_error`;
  - `entry.ci` is saved;
  - AC5 sweep: no note, hand-over line or report holds `READY_PHRASE` on any
    non-green path.
- **D3. The e2e test with a stub `gh` on PATH is missing (AC7).** Add
  `tests/e2e/test_s81_ci_e2e.py` as the design specifies: running
  `CiWatch(GhPrs(command_runner(tmp_path)), 30).results([entry])` against the stub
  gives RED with the `FAILED tests/x.py::test_y` line, and the test checks the
  argv the stub recorded.
- **D4. No round-trip through the state loader.** Two tests are missing:
  - a legacy `state.json` without `ci` or `ci_bug` still loads;
  - a saved `StackEntry.ci` loads back as a `CiResult` through
    `state._upstream_state`.
