## The changed function

### `story_rebase._get_maker(lists, commit)` (s182/AC3, REQ-219)

**What it does now.** It returns `the runner` only when the sha is in `rebase.made` *and*
the subject is not one of `base..old`'s subjects. Otherwise it returns `the role`.

**Defect F1, still open, now with the wrong sense.** The runner makes a commit in exactly
one way: `add_to_tip`, which runs `commit --amend --no-edit` (R3). So every sha in
`rebase.made` keeps the subject of the commit it amended. That gives two cases:

| Tip that was folded | Subject replayed? | Is it ever in a diff line? | `_get_maker` says | Who made the difference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A replayed commit | yes | Only if the role also moved or dropped commits | the role (new) | the role |
| An extra commit `Y` the role added | no | Always, as `extra` | **the runner** | **the role** |

The new condition only touches the first row, which hardly ever shows up. The second row
is the F1 case: the role adds `Y` and leaves an in-scope edit, the runner folds the edit
into `Y'`, and the refusal reads `- extra Y' … (made by the runner)`. That is the s180
mix-up the story is about ("says whether the role or the runner made it"). With R1 in
place, `the runner` can now only be printed when it is wrong.

**Tests.** `test_an_extra_commit_in_made_is_the_runners` (`test_s182_story_rebase.py:70`)
and `test_a_reworded_commit_by_the_runner_says_so` (`:90`) still assert the faulty
outcome. Each one builds a sha in `made` whose subject (`fixup! one`, `uno`) the runner
can't produce under R3. No test covers the new branch (sha in `made`, subject replayed →
`the role`). Line coverage can't see the gap because the whole condition is on one line.

**Fix (developer).** As the second pass said: guard the fold, not the blame. `is_foldable`
(R4) should also require the tip's subject to be one of `base..old`'s subjects. Then a
role-made tip is never amended. Its leftover stays in the worktree, `_find_dirty` refuses
it and names the file (R4's "dirty" path), and `Y` reads `made by the role`. Name the
helper with a house-rules verb (for example `is_tip_replayed`). Script the extra `log`
replies in the fold tests (`test_s182_story_rebase.py`, `test_story_rebase_steps.py`); do
not drop the guard to make them pass. Then either put `_get_maker` back to the design's
rule (R6: runner iff sha in `made`) or keep it. Whichever you choose, rewrite the two tests
above so that the `made` sha has a subject that R3 can actually produce. Add the test the
second pass asked for: a role-made extra tip plus an in-scope leftover gives the dirty
refusal naming the file, and never `made by the runner`. The architect may want R4 to say
this; the rule as written leaves it open.
