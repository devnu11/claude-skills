## Defects

- **D1 (blocking): AC4 and AC-R tests are missing.** Write the design's page checklist
  (§ Test checklist, page items) as a node-harness e2e file against the fixture. Each of
  these is an item there:
  - SN1–SN4: positions inside the subgraphs, hexagons, edges, labelled `linkStyle`
    indices unchanged;
  - SN2: no `st_` node without events, and an open row alone draws one;
  - SN5: the numbers in Run, Story and Sprint scope, plus two runs;
  - SN7/SN8: the running line, `activeNodeId`, the active class, and nothing in idle or replay;
  - SB1–SB3: the share numbers;
  - TM2–TM8;
  - RD1–RD3, measured in both themes;
  - SN1 names against `stages.STAGES`.
- **D2 (test weakened by a gate fix): `test_legend_says_awake_time`.** Gate fix 1613154
  names its rule (TM4/TM8), and the design marks the old test as superseded, so changing
  that assertion is justified. But the design says the new form "pins `Awake time` **and
  the absence of `agent steps only`**". The fix kept only `"Awake time:" in …`. AC2's
  "the legend no longer says 'agent steps only'" is not pinned. Add the negative
  assertion and the five `TIME_PARTS` labels.
