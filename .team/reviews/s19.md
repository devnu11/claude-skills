## D1 (blocking): a done story that gets a hold never gets its Scribe, and nothing can release the hold

How it happens:

1. `Runtime.note_step` sets `story.hold = handover.hold_reason(report)` for
   every story step, including the step that moves the story to `DONE`.
2. `Router._scribe_work` skips held stories (`... if s and not s.hold`).
3. `Tools.continue_story` refuses a done story (`story sN is done`), so the
   PO cannot clear that hold.

Example: the customer-proxy finishes `acceptance` with `done` and either
`open_questions` or a quarantine (both happen often). The story becomes
`DONE`, its id goes into `scribe_due`, and the hold is set to `questions` or
`quarantine`. The PO gets the hand-over but cannot `continue_story` the story.
The Scribe picker then skips the story for good, and `_idle` reports
`SPRINT_END` while the Scribe is still owed. The Scribe silently never runs for
that story, which breaks AC5 ("the scribe runs automatically after each story
reaches done"). The id also stays in `scribe_due` forever.

Each of the three rules is in the design (R2, R6 and the `continue_story`
table), but together they create a dead end. Smallest fix that keeps the
hand-over behaviour: give a `DONE` story no hold in `note_step`. Holds only
affect whether a story can move, and a done story cannot move anyway. The
hand-over still reaches the PO, because triggers come from the report, not the
hold. The PO's turn still runs before the next pass, so the PO handles the
quarantine or questions before the Scribe runs. Another option is for
`_scribe_work` to ignore holds. Either way, add a test: a final-step report
with `done` plus `open_questions` (or a quarantine) hands over, and after the
PO's turn the router runs `scribe` on that story. Note the change in the
design's R6.

## Checklist from the brief

- **Every trigger reaches the PO**: open questions (`questions`),
  blocked/failed (`stopped`), a round cap (`round_cap`) and new human
  messages (`human_message`, checked before every step) each hand over, and
  every matching row is listed, not just the first. Yes.
- **The router never runs a role out of order**: yes (see `step_role`).
- **Holds and `continue_story`**: correct for active stories. Wrong for done
  stories (D1).
- **`set_developer`**: correct, and it ranks first in `ROLE_HINTS`.
- **PO on Sonnet**: the built-in `roles/product-owner.md` has `model: sonnet`.
  This repo's `.team/roles/product-owner.md` only appends text (no
  frontmatter), so it keeps that model. `protocol.md`, `SKILL.md` and the
  README row describe the split.
