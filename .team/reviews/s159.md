## Minor

- `_push_after_cancel`: `task.result()` already raises the settle's error. So the body can
  be `await self.apply_settled(plan, task.result())`, which drops the `if`, the double
  `exception()` call and the `type: ignore` (KISS).
- `_wait_uncut` swallows the cancel without `uncancel()`, then `_settle_uncut` raises a
  fresh `CancelledError`. That's fine for a plain task. Under `asyncio.timeout` or a
  `TaskGroup` the caller's cancel count stays raised, and that is still correct, since a
  cancel does propagate. No change needed. A one-line comment would help the next reader.
- `_own_pipeline` on a view without `pipeline_for` would nest
  (`reports-story/reports-story/<id>`). Nothing calls `at()` on a view today.
