On the N8 design: the note is cleared in `_land`, which runs after any story step settles, so
the role whose step was cut off gets it in its resumed brief and then it's gone. That matches
"until the next step lands". A failed or interrupted step doesn't reach `_land`, so the note
survives into the retry, which is right. If two stashes are told for the same story, the
second overwrites the first; with one stash per story per replay that can't happen. No defect.

The N8 test should check: after `restore_edits` with a gone stash (and again with a kept one),
the story's next brief contains `From the runner:` with the stash sha; after `_land` on that
story the field is `""` and the next brief has no such line; and a stash for a story not in
`state.stories` still posts to the PO and doesn't raise.
