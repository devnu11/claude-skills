# s114: Retire the Scribe and the briefs; the architect writes the ADRs

Developer: **developer-cli** (runner Python, built-in role files, one word in
`dashboard/index.html`, the fixture script). Unit tests: unit-tester. E2E
tests: integration-tester.

The Scribe ran after every done story to write ADRs and a per-role
`.team/briefs/<role>.md` that `roles.system_prompt` pasted into each prompt
as "Brief from the Scribe". Both go. Roles read the story, design, ADRs and
code first-hand, and the Architect records each decision as an ADR while it
designs, when it knows why.

## Words

- **Scribe brief**: `.team/briefs/<role>.md`. This story removes it.
- **Step brief** (the runner's "standard brief", the PO's `run_role` brief,
  `results.BRIEF_RESULTS`, the dashboard Flow chart's "briefs" edge count of
  `step-start` events): unchanged. Do not rename or remove anything about
  step briefs.

## ADR for this story

Written at design review (once `.team/adr/**` was in this repo's architect
scope): `.team/adr/0050-retire-the-scribe-architect-owns-adrs.md`:

- **Context.** The Scribe ran after each done story (Haiku, low effort) and
  rewrote `.team/briefs/<role>.md`, pasted into every prompt. The briefs
  were second-hand, often stale ("docs due" lists that outlived the work),
  and cost a step per story plus prompt tokens on every step. ADRs were
  written after the fact by a role that had not made the decisions.
- **Decision.** Retire the Scribe and the briefs (SC1–SC8). The Architect
  writes and amends ADRs at design (SC9). A retired role name is data
  (`RETIRED_ROLES`), refused on load when configured, ignored as a stub.
- **Consequences.** One fewer step per story; prompts shrink; roles read
  first-hand sources. ADRs are written by the decider. Repos with a
  `[roles.scribe]` or a non-stub `scribe.md` must remove it before the next
  start. Repos that override `[roles.architect] write` must add
  `.team/adr/**`.

## Known limits

- An `interrupted` entry or `in_flight` record from an old run whose role
  is `scribe` still loads; the PO sees it in its resume text, and a
  `run_role("scribe")` is refused as an unknown role. Not worth a migration.
- `docs/adr/ADR-NNN-*.md` (the older architect series) stays as it is; new
  ADRs go to `.team/adr/` only.
- `watch` and the dashboard fall back to a default config on any
  `ConfigError` (existing behaviour), so a `[roles.scribe]` repo shows a
  bare dashboard until it is fixed; `start` and `config check` refuse.
