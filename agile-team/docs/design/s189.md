# s189 design: `remove_from_sprint` takes a not-started story out of the running sprint

Story: `.team/stories/s189.md`. ADR: ADR-0094 (new), ADR-0057 (amended by s189).
Requirements: REQ-252, REQ-253 (AC1), REQ-254 (AC2), REQ-255, REQ-256 (AC3).
Developer: `developer-cli`.

## Cause

A story can leave the running sprint in only three ways: it is done, the human
cancels it, or the PO reverts it. `revert_story` refuses a story with no commits and
no branch (`reverts.NOTHING_TO_REVERT`). `start_sprint` moves unlisted stories to
the Product Backlog, but it opens a new sprint number. So a story that crashed
before its first commit held the sprint end (`sprint_end.end_refusal`) until the
liaison edited `state.json` by hand.

## Decision: a new tool, not a wider `revert_story`

`revert_story` means "take this work out": it resets the story to the step before
tests, adds a revert entry, asks the human the second time, and its next design
needs `## After revert N`. None of that fits a story that never started, and AC1
says the story keeps its step. So the PO gets a new tool, `remove_from_sprint(story,
reason)`, the pair of `add_to_sprint`. `revert_story` does not change.

## Rules

| # | Rule | AC |
|---|------|----|
| L1 | **One story leaves the sprint.** `remove_from_sprint(story, reason)` sets the story's `sprint` to `None`. `state.sprint`, `state.goal`, the sprint branch and the stack entry don't change. No boundary, Manager turn or PO renewal is queued, and no `priority` event is written. The story keeps its `step`, `status`, `rounds`, `depends_on`, developer and branch record. Its id stays where it is in `state.order` and `state.pulled`. `agile-team status` (`stories.<id>.sprint` is `null`) and the Board then show it in the Product Backlog, with no change to either. | AC1 |
| L2 | **Its holds are cleared.** The runner's hold (`story.hold`, any text: a crash, a gate hold, an overlap) becomes `None`, and `story.overlap_with` becomes `[]`. A PO hold of kind `HoldKind.HOLD` becomes `None` too: the PO is the caller, and a hold on a story outside the sprint holds nothing. A `NEEDS_MANAGER` or `BLOCKED` status is kept (it is not a hold, and an open question still names the story). An overlap-held story runs design again when it comes back, and the design gate checks overlap again. | AC1 |
| L3 | **Its worktree goes, its branch stays.** A story with a worktree (`worktree_head` set) gives it up the way a `start_sprint` leaver does (E1, REQ-149): `Workspaces.remove`, `worktree_head = ""`, one `story-branch` event `{"action": "removed", "branch", "base_sha", "replays"}` with role `product-owner`. `start_sprint`'s `_release_leavers` calls the same helper (`sprint_leave.release_worktree`). Kept sessions are left alone, as for a `start_sprint` leaver. | AC1 |
| L4 | **The story file records it.** The runner appends a line under `## Left the sprint` (the heading is added before the first line) in the story file in the main checkout, `- {date}, sprint {sprint}: {reason} Cleared hold: {cleared}.`. `{cleared}` is `runner hold: <hold>`, `PO hold: <reason>`, both joined by `; `, or `none`. It commits that one file as `chore({story}): record the move to the Product Backlog`, holding `runtime.checkout_lock`, then calls `runtime.record_checkout()`. A story with no story file gets no line and `record` is `""` (as `Reverter._record`). | AC1 |
| L5 | **One event and one note.** One `story-admin` event, role `product-owner`: `{"tool": "remove_from_sprint", "reason", "question": null, "sprint": <N>, "cleared": {"hold": <text or null>, "po_hold": <reason or null>}, "record": <sha or "">, "before": facts, "after": facts}` (`story_admin.facts`). The human gets one `update` note: `Story {story} ("{title}") left sprint {sprint} for the Product Backlog: {reason} Cleared hold: {cleared}.` | AC1 |
| L6 | **The result.** `{"status": "removed", "story", "step", "sprint": <N>, "cleared": {…}, "record"}`. `remove_from_sprint` is in `po_tools.STUCK_RESETS`, so a call that is not refused resets `stuck_asks`. | AC1 |
| L7 | **The sprint can end.** `state.sprint_ids()` reads `story.sprint`, so the story leaves `unfinished_in_sprint()`, `END_REFUSED`, the `sprint_backlog_stuck` lines and `keep_going_refusal` with no change to those functions. When the stories left are all done, `sprint_complete()` is true and the end boundary opens on the next pass, as for a revert. | AC2 |
| L8 | **The texts point at it.** `END_REFUSED` becomes `sprint {sprint} can't end: {stories} unfinished. Finish them, revert_story(story, reason), remove_from_sprint(story, reason) for one with no commits, or ask the human to cancel them (request_cancel)`. `SPRINT_STUCK_ADVICE` adds `remove_from_sprint` after `revert_story`: `…, pass_tests, revert_story, remove_from_sprint for a story with no commits, request_cancel, …`. | AC2 |
| L9 | **Refusals, first one wins, nothing changed.** (1) the checkout drifted: the tool returns `halted_report` from `runtime.drift_halt()`, as `revert_story`; (2) a pending boundary: `SprintAdd.boundary_refusal()`'s text, as `revert_story` and `add_to_sprint`; (3) unknown story: `story_admin.UNKNOWN`; (4) blank reason: `story_admin.NO_REASON`; (5) done or cancelled: `story_admin.FINISHED_TEXT` with verb `remove from the sprint`; (6) not in the open sprint (`not state.sprint_open()` or `story.sprint != state.sprint`): `NOT_IN_SPRINT`; (7) a cancel or revert question waits: `story_admin.asked_text`; (8) work (L10); (9) a step runs on the story (`story.id in runtime.flags.running`): `STEP_RUNNING`; (10) unfinished sprint stories list it in `depends_on`: `DEPENDED_ON_IN_SPRINT`. Before the checks the tool settles answered cancel and revert questions (`Clerk.settle`, `Reverter.settle`), as both those tools do. | AC3 |
| L10 | **A story with work is refused and pointed at `revert_story`.** In this order: an open landing (`story.step == "land"`, or `state.landing == story.id`, or a `runtime.revert_queue` call for it): `OPEN_LANDING`; a landing (`story.landed is not None`): `LANDED`; commits (`story.commit is not None` or `story.code_commits`): `HAS_COMMITS`. A branch with no commits on it is not work. | AC3 |

### Texts (constants in `sprint_leave.py`)

```python
NOT_IN_SPRINT = "story {story} is not in the running sprint {sprint}; nothing to remove"
OPEN_LANDING = "story {story} is landing; wait for land to end, then use revert_story(story, reason)"
LANDED = "story {story} has landed; use revert_story(story, reason) to take its work out"
HAS_COMMITS = "story {story} has commits; use revert_story(story, reason) to take its work out"
STEP_RUNNING = "story {story}'s {step} step is running; call again when it ends"
DEPENDED_ON_IN_SPRINT = (
    "unfinished sprint stories depend on {story}: {ids}; take them out first, or keep {story}"
)
VERB = "remove from the sprint"
HEADING = "## Left the sprint"
FILE_LINE = "- {date}, sprint {sprint}: {reason} Cleared hold: {cleared}."
NOTICE = 'Story {story} ("{title}") left sprint {sprint} for the Product Backlog: {reason} Cleared hold: {cleared}.'
RECORD_SUBJECT = "chore({story}): record the move to the Product Backlog"
NO_HOLD = "none"
TOOL = "remove_from_sprint"
```

## Changes per module

### New `agile-team/runner/agile_team/sprint_leave.py`

Docstring: the PO's `remove_from_sprint` (s189): pure rules first, `SprintLeave`
does the state, git, file, event and relay I/O.

Imports: `from . import priority, revert_log, story_admin, story_spec, system`,
`from .dispatch import Runtime, refused`, `from .events import Event, EventKind`,
`from .gates import FINISHED, StoryState`, `from .holds import HoldKind`,
`from .relay import Note`, `from .reverts import Reverter`,
`from .sprint_add import SprintAdd`, `from .state import RunState`,
`from .workspaces import Workspaces`.

- `@dataclass(frozen=True) class Leave`: `story_id: str`, `reason: str`. One call.
- `@dataclass(frozen=True) class Cleared`: `hold: str | None`, `po_hold: str | None`.
  The holds the move clears (L2). `@property text -> str`: the `{cleared}` text of
  L4 (`NO_HOLD` when both are `None`). The event's `cleared` is
  `dataclasses.asdict(cleared)`.
- `read_cleared(story: StoryState) -> Cleared`: `story.hold`, and the PO hold's
  `reason` when its kind is `HoldKind.HOLD`, else `None`.
- `list_sprint_dependents(state: RunState, story_id: str) -> list[str]`: ids in
  `state.unfinished_in_sprint()` whose `depends_on` holds `story_id`, in state order.
- `release_worktree(runtime: Runtime, story: StoryState) -> None`: L3, the body of
  today's `po_tools._release_leavers` loop (no-op when `worktree_head` is empty).
  Role `story_admin.PO_ROLE`.
- `@dataclass class SprintLeave` with `runtime: Runtime`:
  - `async run(self, args: dict[str, Any]) -> dict[str, Any]`: settle (L9), build
    `Leave(str(args.get("story", "")), str(args.get("reason") or ""))`, return
    `refused(why)` on `find_refusal`, else `apply`.
  - `find_refusal(self, leave: Leave) -> str | None`: L9 (2) to (10) through
    `priority.first_refusal`. Checks (5) to (10) read the story, so (3) returns
    first when it is unknown.
  - `async apply(self, leave: Leave) -> dict[str, Any]`: L1 to L6, in this order:
    `before = story_admin.facts(story)`, `cleared = read_cleared(story)`, set
    `sprint`, `hold`, `overlap_with`, `po_hold` (L1, L2), `release_worktree`,
    `runtime.save()`, record (L4), emit the event and post the note (L5), return
    L6's result.
  - private helpers as needed (`_find_work_refusal(story)`, `async _record(story,
    line)` holding `runtime.checkout_lock` around the write, commit and
    `record_checkout`, each under 8 lines). Today's date: `datetime.fromtimestamp(system.now(), UTC)`
    as `reverts._today`.

### `agile-team/runner/agile_team/revert_log.py`

Add one pure helper and make `with_entry` use it (behaviour unchanged):

- `@dataclass(frozen=True) class FileSection`: `heading: str`, `line: str`.
- `append_under(text: str, section: FileSection) -> str`: `text` with a trailing
  newline ensured, `section.heading` added (`\n{heading}\n\n`) when no line of the
  text equals it, then `section.line` and a newline.
- `with_entry(text, entry)` returns `append_under(text, FileSection(HEADING, file_line(entry)))`.

`sprint_leave` writes its line with `append_under(text, FileSection(HEADING, line))`.

### `agile-team/runner/agile_team/po_tools.py`

- `Tools.remove_from_sprint(self, args) -> dict`: docstring "Take a not-started story
  out of the running sprint to the Product Backlog (s189)." Body: `if moved :=
  self.runtime.drift_halt(): return text_result(halted_report(PO, moved))`, then
  `return text_result(await SprintLeave(self.runtime).run(args))`. The tool does
  not take the checkout lock itself; only the record step does (L4).
- `_release_leavers` calls `sprint_leave.release_worktree(rt, story)` in its loop.
- `STUCK_RESETS` gains `"remove_from_sprint"`.
- `SCHEMAS` gains, right after `add_to_sprint`:

```python
"remove_from_sprint": (
    "Take one story that has no commits and no landing out of the running sprint, "
    "back to the Product Backlog, with a reason: the sprint keeps its number, goal and "
    "branch; the story keeps its step and its place in the order. Its holds (a crash, "
    "a gate hold, your own hold) are cleared and recorded in the story file and the "
    "events, and the human is told. Use it for a story that never started, e.g. one "
    "that crashed before its first commit. Refused, with nothing changed, for unknown, "
    "done or cancelled stories, a blank reason, a story not in the sprint, a pending "
    "cancel or revert question, a story with commits, a landing or an open landing "

    "and while a boundary is pending.",
    {"story": str, "reason": str},
),
```
