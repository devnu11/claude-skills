# s180 design: the runner's own commits are carried over a green landing

Story: `.team/stories/s180.md`. Requirements: REQ-190 (AC1, AC2), REQ-191
(AC1), REQ-192 (AC3). REQ-164 and REQ-169 are changed to name the exception.
ADR: ADR-0090 (new), ADR-0084 amended.
Developer: `developer-cli`.

## The decision in one paragraph

The story left two options open: defer the runner's bookkeeping commits
while a landing waits, or carry them over the landing. This design carries
them. At green, under the main lock, the commits that moved the sprint head
since the merge was made are cherry-picked onto the merge in the land
worktree. The remote sprint branch moves to the merge (CI-green, same sha),
and the main checkout moves to the last copy. Deferring would need a wait at
six different commit sites (story intake, sharing, reverts, PO tools, PO
settles, storyless steps), some in the middle of a PO tool call. A held
landing would also stall story intake for hours. See ADR-0090.

## Rules

- **C1. The move.** `carry.Move` describes how the head moved: `merge`;
  `base`, the merge's first parent (`git rev-parse <merge>^1`, `""` when that
  fails); `head`, `Lander.sprint_head` now; `pushed`, the sprint entry's
  `pushed` (`""` with no entry); and `forks`, a `(story id, sha)` pair for
  every story's non-empty `base_sha` and every queued `rebase.onto`. The span
  is `base..head`. `is_unmoved` is `base == head`.
- **C2. When a move is carried.** `carry.find_refusal(git, move)` runs the
  `REFUSALS` table in order and returns the first refusal text, or `None`
  when the move can be carried. Callers only call it when the move is not
  unmoved.
  1. `find_not_above`: `merge-base --is-ancestor <base> <head>` exits
     non-zero, or `rev-list <span>` is empty. Text: `NOT_ABOVE`.
  2. `find_merge`: `log --merges --format=%h %s <span>` has a line, meaning
     another landing or a revert landing moved the head. Text: `MERGED`
     with the first line.
  3. `find_pushed`: `pushed` is set and
     `merge-base --is-ancestor <pushed> <merge>` exits non-zero, meaning the
     remote already has a moved commit. Text: `PUSHED`.
  4. `find_fork`: a `forks` sha is in `rev-list <span>`. Text: `FORKED`,
     naming the first such story and sha. A branch forked on a moved commit
     would keep the old copies, and the sprint end's replay would see them
     as story commits.
- **C3. Carrying.** `carry.carry_commits(tree, move)` runs in the land
  worktree, detached at the merge. It reads `old = rev-list --reverse <span>`,
  then runs `git cherry-pick --allow-empty --keep-redundant-commits <span>`.
  On a non-zero exit it reads the conflicting files
  (`diff --name-only --diff-filter=U`), runs `cherry-pick --abort`, and
  returns `STOPPED`. On success it reads
  `new = rev-list --reverse <merge>..HEAD`. When the counts differ it returns
  `UNEVEN`. Otherwise it returns `Carried(tip=HEAD, head=move.head,
  moved=dict(zip(old, new)))`. Cherry-pick keeps each commit's author, date,
  message and trailers (`Requirement-Changed`, the story authorship
  trailers).
- **C4. Green with a carry.** In `push_and_follow` (pushed) and
  `land_locally` (local), under the main lock: plan the carry. A refusal
  gives up the merge as today (`why: "sprint head moved"`), with the refusal
  recorded. Otherwise push the sprint branch to the **merge** (lease
  `entry.pushed`, as today), set `entry.pushed = merge`, then move the main
  checkout:
  - nothing moved: `upstream.advance_to(merge)`, as today;
  - a carry: `upstream.reset_team_branch(carried.head, carried.tip)`
    (`reset --keep`, refuses when HEAD isn't `carried.head`), then
    `rewrite.remap_state(state, carried.moved)`. An `UpstreamError` counts
    as a failed move and halts as checkout drift, as a failed fast-forward
    does today.
  Then `record_checkout()`. That sets `state.checkout` and the entry's
  `head` to the tip. The carried copies stay unpushed. The next landing's
  merge or the sprint end's push takes them to the remote, which is where
  they were before s180.
- **C5. Resuming.** `check_resume` gives up a pushed landing only when the
  move is not unmoved and `find_refusal` refuses. A carryable move resumes
  the CI wait; the carry happens at green (C4). A conflict is only found at
  green; then it is a drop as in C4.
- **C6. No fork during a moved landing (REQ-191).** `RunFlags.landing_base`
  is the in-flight landing merge's first parent, or `""`. The lander sets it
  to `attempt.head` once `merge()` made the merge, and to `move.base` in
  `check_resume`. It clears it in `give_up` and when `land`/`land_revert`
  end (`finally`). `Router._story_offers` skips a story when
  `_is_held_by_landing(story)`: `landing_base` is set, the story isn't at
  `land`, it has no `worktree_head`, and `landing_base != runtime.sprint_head`.
  Such a story would fork or replay its branch onto a moved commit. It is
  offered again once the landing ends. If the head never moved, nothing
  waits.
- **C7. The records.** `story-landed` gains `carried`: `{old sha: new sha}`
  (`{}` when nothing was carried). `remap_state` rewrites what it already
  covers: `file_commits`, `code_commits`, `commit`, interruptions, revert
  entries and the stack's `start`/`end`/`last_end`. C2.4 guarantees that no
  `base_sha` is in the span. Events, liaison notes and `story_requests` texts
  keep the old shas; `carried` maps them. Old commits stay in the object
  store, so an old quarantine sha still cherry-picks.
- **D1. A drop names what moved (REQ-192).** `give_up` adds to the
  `landing-dropped` data `moved`, the `carry.list_moved` lines
  (`log --reverse --format=%h %s <span>`, every commit, not only first
  parents, oldest first), and `not_carried`, the refusal text or `""`. The
  data now holds `merge, why, sprint_head, moved, not_carried`. It also
  appends `{"merge", "why", "moved"}` to `attempt.dropped`. A refused sprint
  push (`why: "sprint push refused"`) lists its span the same way, usually
  empty.
- **D2. The PO hears of a drop.** `run_attempts` puts `attempt.dropped`
  into the report it returns, as `report["land"]["dropped"]` (only when
  there was a drop). A new story-scoped trigger `landing_dropped` matches it.
  Its line names each dropped merge, its `why` and the moved commits. The
  final outcome (landed, bounced, held) still gets its own lines.
- **N1. Unchanged.** The remote only ever gets CI-green landing merges (and
  the boundary's pushes). Landings stay one at a time, so one
  `landing_base` is enough. A merge in the span, a rewritten head and a
  refused push still drop as in s163. `MAX_RESTARTS` stays 5.

## Changes per module

### New `agile-team/runner/agile_team/carry.py`

Module docstring: `"""Carrying the runner's own commits over a green landing (s180, ADR-0090)."""`
plus one sentence on the why (see ADR-0090's context). Imports:
`dataclasses.dataclass, field`, `.git_ops.Git`.

Constants (texts are exact):

```
NOT_ABOVE = "the sprint head does not descend from the merge's first parent"
MERGED = "a merge moved the sprint head: {line}"
PUSHED = "the remote sprint branch is at {pushed}, which the merge does not contain"
FORKED = "story {story}'s branch starts on the moved commit {sha}"
STOPPED = "carrying the moved commits stopped on a conflict in {files}"
UNEVEN = "carrying {old} moved commits made {new}"
LINE_FORMAT = "--format=%h %s"
```

`{pushed}` and `{sha}` are cut to 12 characters. `{files}` is joined with `", "`.

```python
@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Move:
    """How the sprint head moved since a landing's merge was made (C1)."""
    merge: str
    base: str
    head: str
    pushed: str = ""
    forks: tuple[tuple[str, str], ...] = ()

    @property
    def span(self) -> str:            # "<base>..<head>"
    @property
    def is_unmoved(self) -> bool:     # base == head

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Carried:
    """Where the sprint branch ends after a green landing, and the copies made (C3)."""
    tip: str
    head: str
    moved: dict[str, str] = field(default_factory=dict)
```

Functions, each with a one-line docstring, at most 2 parameters:

- `list_moved(git: Git, move: Move) -> list[str]`: the `%h %s` lines of
  `git log --reverse <LINE_FORMAT> <span>`, blank lines dropped (D1). Uses
  `git.attempt`. A failure gives `[]`.
- `list_shas(git: Git, span: str) -> list[str]`: `rev-list --reverse <span>`
  lines (`git.attempt`, `[]` on failure).
- `find_not_above(git: Git, move: Move) -> str | None`, `find_merge(...)`,
  `find_pushed(...)`, `find_fork(...)`: C2.1 to C2.4. Each returns its text or
  `None`.
- `REFUSALS = (find_not_above, find_merge, find_pushed, find_fork)`: a
  module constant after the four functions.
- `find_refusal(git: Git, move: Move) -> str | None`: the first non-`None`
  of `REFUSALS`, else `None`.
- `carry_commits(tree: Git, move: Move) -> Carried | str`: C3. Uses
  `tree.attempt` for the cherry-pick. Keep it short, with the failure branch
  in `stop_carry(tree: Git) -> str` (reads the `U` files, aborts, returns
  `STOPPED`).

### `agile-team/runner/agile_team/landing.py`

- Imports: `from . import carry, rewrite` (add to the existing `from . import`
  line). `UpstreamError` is already imported.
- `Attempt` gains `not_carried: str = ""`,
  `dropped: list[dict[str, Any]] = field(default_factory=list)` and
  `carried: dict[str, str] = field(default_factory=dict)`. Import `field`.
- **Remove** `is_on_head` (its two callers move to the carry).
- `land`: wrap the body so that `finally` sets
  `self.runtime.flags.landing_base = ""`. Do the same in `land_revert`.
- `run_land`: unchanged, but `check_resume` changes:
  `async def check_resume(self, attempt: Attempt) -> None`, docstring
  `"""G1, C5: resume a pushed landing while its merge is on the head or the move can be carried."""`.
  Read the move, set `flags.landing_base = move.base`, and when the move is
  not unmoved and `find_refusal` (in a thread) refuses: set
  `attempt.not_carried`, then `give_up(attempt, GIVE_UP_MOVED)`.
- `run_attempt`: after `merge` returns `None`, set
  `self.runtime.flags.landing_base = attempt.head`.
- `run_attempts`: both returns go through
  `self.add_drops(attempt, report)`.
- New `def add_drops(self, attempt: Attempt, report: dict[str, Any]) -> dict[str, Any]`:
  `"""D2: the report with the attempt's drops under land.dropped, if any."""`.
  With no drops, return the report unchanged. Otherwise do
  `report.setdefault("land", {})["dropped"] = list(attempt.dropped)`.
- New `async def read_move(self, attempt: Attempt) -> carry.Move`:
  `"""C1: the merge's first parent, the sprint head, the pushed record and the story forks."""`.
  Run `rev-parse <merge>^1` through `run_off(git.attempt, ...)`; use `""` when
  it fails. `forks` comes from `state.stories`: each `base_sha` and each
  `rebase.onto` that is set, paired with the story id, in story order. A
  small sync helper `find_forks(self) -> tuple[tuple[str, str], ...]` is
  fine. It reads state, so make it a property `forks` if it takes nothing.
- New `async def plan_carry(self, attempt: Attempt) -> carry.Carried | str`:
  `"""C2, C3: the tip to move to and the carried copies, or why the move can't be carried."""`.
  An unmoved move gives `Carried(attempt.merge, move.head)`. Otherwise a
  refusal is returned, else `await self.carry_in_tree(move)`.
- New `async def carry_in_tree(self, move: carry.Move) -> carry.Carried | str`:
  `clear_tree`, `worktree add --detach <scratch> <merge>`, then
  `carry.carry_commits(Git(self.scratch_path), move)` in a thread. Always
  `clear_tree` after, in a `finally`.
- New `async def take_carry(self, attempt: Attempt) -> carry.Carried | None`:
  `"""C4: plan the carry; on a refusal give up the merge and return None."""`.
- New `async def move_checkout(self, attempt: Attempt, carried: carry.Carried) -> bool`:
  `"""C4: fast-forward to the merge, or reset to the carried tip and remap the run's shas."""`.
  Nothing moved means `advance_to(carried.tip)`. Otherwise call
  `reset_team_branch(carried.head, carried.tip)` in a thread; an
  `UpstreamError` returns False. Then set `attempt.carried = carried.moved`,
  `rewrite.remap_state(self.runtime.state, carried.moved)` and return True.
- `land_locally`: under the lock, `carried = await self.take_carry(attempt)`.
  `None` returns `None` (a fresh start), as today. Then `move_checkout`
  (False halts with `halt_diverged(attempt, "")`), then `record_checkout()`.
- `push_and_follow`: `take_carry` replaces the `is_on_head` check. Push the
  merge with the lease as today. A refusal goes to `follow_remote`. Then
  `entry.pushed = attempt.merge`, then `move_checkout` (False halts as
  today), then `record_checkout()`. The carry runs **before** the push, so a
  refused carry pushes nothing.
- `give_up(self, attempt: Attempt, why: str) -> None` keeps its signature.
  It adds `moved` (from `carry.list_moved` over `read_move`, in a thread) and
  `not_carried` to the event data, appends `{"merge", "why", "moved"}` to
  `attempt.dropped`, resets `attempt.not_carried = ""` and clears
  `flags.landing_base`. Split the data into a helper, e.g.
  `async def note_drop(self, attempt: Attempt, why: str) -> dict[str, Any]`,
  to keep each function short.
- `record_landed`: `data["carried"] = dict(attempt.carried)`.

## Test checklist

Unit tests (fake git, no real OS):
`tests/test_carry.py` (module `carry`) and `tests/test_s180_landing_carry.py`
(the lander, router and hand-over), built on `test_s159_landing.setup`,
`fake.reply` and `issued` as in `test_s163_landing_moves.py`.

C1, C2 (REQ-190):
- [ ] `Move.span` is `base..head`, and `is_unmoved` is true only when they
      are equal.
- [ ] `find_refusal` returns `NOT_ABOVE` when `merge-base --is-ancestor`
      fails and when `rev-list` is empty, `MERGED` with the first `%h %s`
      line when `log --merges` prints one, `PUSHED` when `pushed` isn't an
      ancestor of the merge, `FORKED` with the story id and sha when a fork
      sha is in the span. It returns `None` when all pass. With an empty
      `pushed` it doesn't run the pushed check.
- [ ] The table order holds: a span that is both merged and forked gives
      `MERGED`.

C3:
- [ ] `carry_commits` issues `cherry-pick --allow-empty
      --keep-redundant-commits base..head`. On success it returns
      `Carried(tip, head, {old: new})` paired in order.
- [ ] A failed cherry-pick reads the `U` files, runs `cherry-pick --abort`
      and returns `STOPPED` naming them.
- [ ] Unequal counts return `UNEVEN`.
- [ ] `list_moved` returns the `%h %s` lines oldest first, and `[]` when git
      fails.

C4, C5 (REQ-190), Lander:
- [ ] Green with a carryable move: the sprint push names the **merge**
      (`<merge>:refs/heads/<sprint>`), `reset --keep <tip>` runs and
      `advance_to` doesn't, `entry.pushed == merge`, the report passes with
      the same merge, `story-landed` has `carried` = the pairs, and no
      `landing-dropped` is written.
- [ ] `remap_state` ran: a story's `file_commits` holding a moved sha now
      holds its copy.
- [ ] Green with nothing moved: as before, `advance_to(merge)` and
      `carried == {}`. The s163 probe test still sees `advance_to` and
      `record_checkout` under the lock.
- [ ] Green with a refused move: no sprint push, `landing-dropped` with
      `why: "sprint head moved"` and `not_carried` = the refusal text.
- [ ] A carry that stops on a conflict: no sprint push, a drop with
      `STOPPED` in `not_carried`, and the land worktree is removed.
- [ ] `reset_team_branch` raising `UpstreamError` halts as checkout drift,
      after the push, with `entry.pushed == merge`.
- [ ] Local mode carries the same way: `reset --keep`, no push.
- [ ] A resumed pushed landing (`story.landed.pushed`) with a carryable move
      makes no new merge (`merge --no-ff` not issued) and no drop. With a
      refused move it drops once and merges afresh.

C6 (REQ-191):
- [ ] `flags.landing_base` is the merge's first parent while the land runs
      (set after the merge and in `check_resume`) and `""` after `land` and
      `land_revert` return, and after a drop.
- [ ] `Router.list_works` leaves out a story with no `worktree_head` while
      `landing_base` is set and differs from `sprint_head`. It offers the
      story when `landing_base` is `""` or equals the sprint head, or when
      the story has a worktree. A story at `land` is never left out by this
      rule.

D1, D2 (REQ-192):
- [ ] `landing-dropped` data holds `moved` (the `%h %s` lines of
      `base..head`) and `not_carried`.
- [ ] A land that dropped once and then landed returns a report with
      `land.dropped == [{"merge", "why", "moved"}]`. A land with no drop has
      no `dropped` key.
- [ ] `handover.triggers` on that report includes `landing_dropped`, and
      `report_lines` gives a line naming the 12-character merge, `sprint head
      moved` and each moved line. With no moved lines it says
      `nothing listed`.
- [ ] `SCOPES[Trigger.LANDING_DROPPED] is Scope.STORY`.

End to end (real git), `tests/e2e/test_s180_carry_e2e.py`, on the
`test_s159_landing_e2e` scene as in `test_s163_resume_e2e.py`:
- [ ] AC1: land s1 with pending CI (holds, `landed.pushed` set). Commit a

- [ ] AC1 during the wait, not only on resume: a `ShaWatch` stub that makes

- [ ] AC2: the same with a storyless-style commit (any non-story file, the

- [ ] AC3: s1 held on pending CI, then s2 lands (a merge moves the head).
      s1's next land drops with `moved` listing `merge(s2): …` among its
