## Rules

- **R1 Who writes stories.** Every doc says the same thing. The liaison
  writes every story and sends it with `agile-team story new` or `story
  amend`. The runner commits it between steps. No role writes a story file,
  and the PO schedules stories and asks for them with `request_story`. No
  doc tells anyone to write a story file by hand or to call `open_story`.
  The token `open_story` appears in none of `SKILL.md`, `protocol.md`,
  `liaison-template.md`, `roles/product-owner.md` or the README.
- **R2 Fixing a story file** (ADR-0070 amendment):
  - wrong ACs or environment: the liaison runs `story amend`, and the story
    goes back to design;
  - an environment lost from the config: fix the config, then
    `continue_story`;
  - a wrong `title` or `depends_on`: the human edits and commits the file
    with the run stopped.
- **R3 What the PO asks for.** `request_story` is for bugs, because it
  requires failing output. For a missing check, the ops-review row counts
  as the output. For any other story the PO needs, it asks the human with
  `ask_user`.
- **R4 A CI hold's bug story** is sent with `--sprint next`, because the
  boundary is open and a `current` add would be refused.
- **R5 Kept phrases.** The PO role-file edit must keep every phrase that
  existing tests pin:
  - `test_s110_ci_rule.py`: the CI rule text; in the `**ci_failed**` bullet,
    within 900 characters of its start, `read the failing lines first`, `no
    runner was assigned` and ``Never `continue_boundary` hoping a re-run
    turns green``; `never tell the human the sprint is done`;
  - `test_s114.py` and `e2e/test_s117_e2e.py`: the mission's
    `sends a story back with\nthe gate's reason, and runs the manager` and
    item 5 exactly as it is;
  - `test_roles.py`: `continue_story`, `set_developer`, `standard brief`,
    `judgement`, `point at artifacts rather than restating them`, `the
    runner sends it back to e2e`;
  - `test_s51.py`: `Started stories (with commits) always go first`;
  - `test_s83_backlog.py`: `From the dashboard`;
  - `e2e/test_lean_context_e2e.py`: `.team/po/**`.
