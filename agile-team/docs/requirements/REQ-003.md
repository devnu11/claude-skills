---
id: REQ-003
title: A story leaves design only with every AC traced
status: active
covers: ["s1/AC3", "s1/AC6", "s1/AC7"]
---

When the architect finishes the design of a story designed from s1 on, the
runner checks that each of the story's ACs is covered by an active
requirement and that every requirement file naming the story is sound. If
not, the story stays at design, and the architect gets every reason at once.
Stories designed before s1 are not checked. The product's own requirements
start traced: s1's ACs are covered from the first day.
