---
id: REQ-196
title: A red story CI's event and the PO's hand-over list each failing check with its step, lines and link
status: active
covers: ["s172/AC1"]
---

When a story's PR CI is red, the `story-ci` event's `checks` and the PO's
`story_ci_red` hand-over list every check that did not pass in the form the
sprint CI uses: `- <label>: fail at step <step> <link>`, with the check's
failing log lines indented below it. The runner fetches the job and its
failed log at most once per commit and check, and still reads a story's CI
at most once every 60 seconds.
