---
id: REQ-016
title: A CI failure where our code ran blocks the boundary and can't be waved through
status: active
covers: ["s111/AC2", "s111/AC6"]
---

When a required check fails and its failed job ran steps, the boundary holds
`ci_failed`, and `continue_boundary` there is refused whatever the note says
("flaky" is no reason). The refusal names the job and the failing step and
says to open a bug story or route the fix to an open one with
`record_ci_bug`. A test pins the refusal.
