---
id: REQ-187
title: time_left is measured when the command runs and changes nothing else
status: active
covers: ["s162/AC2"]
---

`time_left` is computed from the job's stored `deadline` against the clock at
the moment the command runs. Reporting it writes no new state, to
`state.json` or to `events.jsonl`, and every other field of the report keeps
its value and its place.
