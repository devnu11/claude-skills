---
id: REQ-169
title: A landing never pushes a merge the sprint head has moved past, except over the runner's own carried commits
status: active
covers: ["s163/AC3"]
---

A pushed landing is resumed (its CI read again at the same sha, with no new
merge) while its merge's first parent is still the sprint head. It is also
resumed when the head moved past the merge's first parent only by plain,
unpushed commits that no open story was forked from (REQ-234). Those commits
are then carried onto the merge, and the sprint push sends the merge itself.
Otherwise the landing gives up its merge: the landing record is cleared, the
landing branch is deleted, and the story merges afresh onto the current
sprint head with new checks and CI. A refused sprint push acts on the fetched
remote head (s177): it holds when the remote didn't move (REQ-267), counts as
pushed when the remote equals the merge (REQ-268), gives up its merge and
lands again when the remote moved on from the runner's record (REQ-269), and
halts as checkout drift when the remote doesn't descend from the record.
