---
id: REQ-236
title: A landing dropped because the sprint head moved names every commit that moved it
status: active
covers: ["s180/AC3"]
---

Sometimes the commits that moved the sprint head can't be carried onto the
landing's merge: the head no longer descends from the merge's base, one of
them is a merge or is already pushed, an open story was forked from one of
them, or replaying them conflicts. The landing then gives up its merge as
before. Its `landing-dropped` event lists every commit between the merge's
first parent and the sprint head, oldest first, as a short sha and a
subject, and says why the carry was refused. The land step's report carries
the same list. The PO gets a `landing_dropped` hand-over line naming those
commits. The line holds nothing.
