---
id: REQ-236
title: A landing dropped because the sprint head moved names every commit that moved it
status: active
covers: ["s180/AC3"]
---

Sometimes the commits that moved the sprint head can't be carried onto the
landing's merge: the merge's first parent can't be read, the head no longer
descends from the merge's base, one of them is a merge or is already pushed,
an open story was forked from one of them, or replaying them conflicts. The
landing then gives up its merge as before. Its `landing-dropped` event lists
every commit between the merge's first parent and the sprint head, oldest
first, as a short sha and a subject, and says why the carry was refused.
When the first parent can't be read, there is no span to name: the list is
empty and the reason says the first parent can't be read. The land step's report carries
the same list. The PO gets a `landing_dropped` hand-over line naming those
commits. The line holds nothing.
