title: A start ledgers, once, the spend a crash cut off, estimated from turns.jsonl
status: active
covers: ["s10/AC1", "s10/AC2"]
---

When the runner stops before a query's ledger row is written, the next `start` adds
one ledger row for each role and story whose model turns in `.team/run/turns.jsonl`
came after its latest ledger row. The row's cost and tokens are the sum of those turns'
estimates. A later start finds those turns covered and adds nothing, and a query the
ledger already holds is never added again. A test fakes a crash in the middle of a PO
turn and pins this.
