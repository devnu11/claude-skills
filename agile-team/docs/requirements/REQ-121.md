title: The PO's role file, protocol.md and SKILL.md describe the stuck Sprint Backlog rule
status: active
covers: ["s156/AC8"]
---

The PO's role file replaces its "ready backlog" hand-over bullet with the
stuck Sprint Backlog bullet and its actions. Its Cadence section no longer
advises `add_to_sprint` for a stuck sprint. `reference/protocol.md` (the
hand-over list, the waiting and `until-blocker` bullets, "Backlog and sprints")
and `SKILL.md` say that a stuck Sprint Backlog is reported, never refilled from
the Product Backlog, and that 3 stuck hand-overs with no PO action block the run.
