---
id: REQ-168
title: A design names its files, and a design that overlaps open work waits at design
status: active
covers: ["s163/AC2"]
---

Every design lists the files its story changes in a `## Files` section, one
path or glob per line, and the design gate bounces a design without one. When
a story passes design while another open story in the sprint is past design,
the runner compares the two Files lists and the files the other story's
branch has changed since its base. On an overlap the story stays at design,
held as `overlap`. The PO gets a hand-over naming both stories, the files
each changes and the overlap. The runner lifts the hold once every
overlapping story has landed, left the sprint or been cancelled.
`release_story` lifts it early, and only with a reason, which is recorded.
