title: add_to_sprint stays a deliberate PO action and is never the advice for a stuck Sprint Backlog
status: active
covers: ["s156/AC5", "s156/AC7"]
---

The PO can still add stories to the running sprint with `add_to_sprint(stories,
reason)`; a reason is still required. No runner hand-over, refusal or PO role
text suggests `add_to_sprint` as the answer to a stuck Sprint Backlog, and the
sprint-end refusal no longer ends with that advice. A test pins this.
