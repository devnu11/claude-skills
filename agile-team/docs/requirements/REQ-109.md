---
id: REQ-109
title: A unit test that requests a temp-dir fixture is refused before any fixture runs
status: active
covers: ["s154/AC2", "s154/AC4"]
---

A unit test (outside `tests/e2e/`) that requests `tmp_path`,
`tmp_path_factory`, `tmpdir` or `tmpdir_factory` is found at collection and
fails at setup, before any of its fixtures runs, with the guard's advice: use
the fake world, or move the test to `tests/e2e/`. The rest of the session runs.
Tests under `tests/e2e/` may request them. Tests pin the refusal for each name
and show that no fixture of a refused test ran.
