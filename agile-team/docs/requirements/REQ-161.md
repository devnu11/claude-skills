---
id: REQ-161
title: A hold or cancel takes effect at a safe point, and an interrupted story step keeps its worktree and session
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC11.1", "s159/AC11.2"]
---

A hold on a story takes effect when its running step ends. A cancel stops the
running step between tool calls, never during a commit. An interrupted story
step is recorded on its story with its session, and its worktree is kept as
it is. On resume, the step is marked stopped, and the next step continues
from the worktree and the saved session. The commit-and-revert salvage
applies only to storyless steps.
