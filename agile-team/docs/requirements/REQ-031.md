---
id: REQ-031
title: When a step ends its box counts it once, from the ledger
status: active
covers: ["s137/AC2", "s137/AC3"]
---

When the running step ends, the role's box switches from the step's live
numbers to the ledger's and the finished step's time, without counting the
step twice. The runner writes the ledger row before the step's end event, so
the live tokens and dollars stop counting as soon as the ledger holds the
step, and the live time stops when the step's end is recorded. Tests pin that
no number is counted twice, both while the step is being settled and after it
has ended.
