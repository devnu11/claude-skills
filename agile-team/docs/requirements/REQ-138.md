---
id: REQ-138
title: The sprint end replays the sprint onto main as story merges on a new branch, and main moves only by fast-forward
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC2.5", "s159/AC2.8", "s159/AC2.12"]
---

At the sprint end, after the as-built push and its green CI, the runner
replays the sprint branch onto main along its first-parent line. Each story's
commits are rebased onto the new tip and merged again with `--no-ff` and the
same message. A plain commit is cherry-picked. The replay goes to a new
branch `<prefix><N>-r<k>` with a ready PR. The stack entry keeps the as-built
branch's record, and the old draft PR is closed with a link to the new one.
main moves only by a fast-forward to the replayed branch. The PR body and the
reports give that command, never "Rebase and merge". At a sprint start, a
replay onto a moved main goes to the next `-r<k>` branch when the newest PR is
still open, and is only local otherwise.
