---
id: REQ-115
title: A stuck Sprint Backlog hands the PO each story's stall reason and offers no Product Backlog stories
status: active
covers: ["s156/AC1", "s156/AC7"]
---

When nothing in the Sprint Backlog can move and no question is open, the runner
hands the PO a `sprint_backlog_stuck` hand-over. It lists each unfinished Sprint
Backlog story with its step and why it can't move, then advice on the actions
the PO may take on those stories. It lists no Product Backlog story and never
advises `add_to_sprint`. The `ready_backlog` hand-over no longer exists. While a
question is open, the runner waits for the human instead. A test pins this.
