---
id: REQ-240
title: An older state.json with no story-branch fields loads and resumes without removing a worktree or halting
status: active
covers: ["s185/AC2"]
---

A `state.json` written before story branches (no `story_branches`, and no
`branch`, `base_sha`, `worktree_head`, `pushed`, `pr`, `replays` or
`landed` on its stories) still loads, and `start --resume` from it goes
on under REQ-239's rules. No worktree is removed and no `worktree-pruned`
event is written for a story that has no worktree recorded, and the resume
does not halt the run because the fields are missing.
