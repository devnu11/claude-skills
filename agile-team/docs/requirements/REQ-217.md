---
id: REQ-217
title: A finished story rebase's uncommitted in-scope edit is folded into the branch's last commit and the branch is adopted
status: active
covers: ["s182/AC1"]
---

When a story rebase step hands off done with the rebase finished, on its
branch and on the sprint head, and leaves an edit to one of its conflicting
files uncommitted, the runner adds that edit to the rebased branch's last
commit. The commit keeps its message. The runner adds no commit of its own,
and the story adopts the branch with the edit on it. The step's report names
the file. A rebase that the edit can't be folded into is bounced with a
reason that names the file, never as a changed-commits refusal.
