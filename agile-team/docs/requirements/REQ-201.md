---
id: REQ-201
title: No story branch starts on a commit a waiting landing would carry
status: active
covers: ["s180/AC1"]
---

While a landing holds a merge (made, checked, pushed or waiting on CI) and
the sprint head has moved past that merge's first parent, a story that has
no branch yet doesn't start: its branch isn't forked onto the moved head.
This holds while the landing is held on CI and after the run restarts. It
starts once the landing ends, on the new sprint head. A story that already
has a branch carries on as before. So a story filed during a landing's CI
wait doesn't cost that landing its merge.
