---
id: REQ-201
title: No story branch starts on a commit a waiting landing would carry
status: active
covers: ["s180/AC1"]
---

While a landing holds a merge (made, checked, pushed or waiting on CI) and
the sprint head has moved past that merge's first parent, a story that has
no worktree yet doesn't start: its branch isn't forked or replayed onto the
moved head. It starts once the landing ends, on the new sprint head. A story
that already has a worktree carries on as before. So a story filed during a
landing's CI wait doesn't cost that landing its merge.
