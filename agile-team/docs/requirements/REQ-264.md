---
id: REQ-264
title: A committed config that fails to parse or validate keeps the last good config, is logged with its error, and reaches the committing step's hand-over or the PO
status: active
covers: ["s173/AC3"]
---

When the newly committed `.agile-team.toml` doesn't parse, breaks a config
rule, or names a role the runner can't resolve, the run goes on with the
last good config and stops nothing. The runner writes one `config` event
holding the validation error, once for that content.

The error reaches the PO's hand-over, and nothing is held. When the step
whose own commit made the change has just ended, the error is in that step's
report. Otherwise (an outside commit, or a landing that brought the change
in) it comes as a hand-over of its own. A later valid commit is taken as
usual.
