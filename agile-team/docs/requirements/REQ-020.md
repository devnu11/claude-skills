---
id: REQ-020
title: A design file matches the rulings and what its story shipped
status: active
covers: ["s87/AC1", "s87/AC2"]
---

A story's design file does not ask for work that a human ruling cancelled or
that the story did not ship. A cancelled row is removed or marked void with
the ruling that voided it. A rule the story shipped differently gets a dated
amendment that says what shipped and why, so a later story does not build
on it.
