---
id: REQ-039
title: A story's commit says who wrote it and how much the human was involved
status: active
covers: ["s135/AC3", "s135/AC6"]
---

The commit that creates a story has the subject `docs(<id>): created story to
<title>`. With `--approved-by none`, its author is the liaison. With
`--approved-by human`, its author is the repo's git user, the human. The
message body has a `Human-involvement:` line: for example "approved plan
`<path>`", or "none: written and submitted by the liaison". Tests pin both
authorship modes.
