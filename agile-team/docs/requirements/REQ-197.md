---
id: REQ-197
title: A failed lint, format or house-rules step shows its finding lines; a test step shows its FAILED lines
status: active
covers: ["s172/AC2"]
---

The failing lines picked from a failed CI step name what failed. A test step
shows its `FAILED`, `ERROR …::` and `--- FAIL:` lines and its count line. A
step with no such line shows its finding lines: ruff's and house-rules'
`path:line: message` lines, ruff's two-line findings joined into one, `Would
reformat:` lines, and error annotations. Warnings are not shown. Only a log
with none of these falls back to its last lines. This applies to sprint CI,
landing CI and story CI alike.
