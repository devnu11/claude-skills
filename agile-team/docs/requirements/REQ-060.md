id: REQ-060
title: The human and the liaison ask for a pick of a commit or a range, and a bad ask is refused with every reason
status: active
covers: ["s141/AC1.1", "s141/AC1.2", "s141/AC1.3"]
---
`agile-team pick <commit> --reason "<text>"` queues one pick request for one
commit, and `agile-team pick <A>..<B> --reason "<text>"` queues one request
for every commit in the range, oldest first. The runner never fetches, so
whoever asks makes sure the commits are in the local repo. A request is
refused at once, with nothing queued, when any of its commits is not in the
local repo, is a merge commit, or has a patch already on the team branch.
The refusal lists every reason.
