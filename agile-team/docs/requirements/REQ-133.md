---
id: REQ-133
title: Drift in a story's worktree blocks only that story until accept-checkout --story keeps it
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC1.4"]
---

The checkout guard checks a story's worktree before each of its steps. Drift
there blocks only that story, names the changed files, and hands over to the
PO as `worktree_drift`. `agile-team accept-checkout --story <id>` works while
the runner runs. It leaves a request that the runner applies between steps,
recording the worktree's head and clearing the block. Drift in the main
checkout still halts the run, and `accept-checkout` without `--story` works
as before.
