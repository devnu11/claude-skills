---
id: REQ-234
title: A story-file commit made while a landing waits on CI doesn't cost that landing its merge
status: active
covers: ["s180/AC1"]
---

A story filed or amended (by `agile-team story new` or `story amend`, or by
the PO's tools) moves the sprint head with a commit while a landing is pushed
and waiting for its CI. When that CI comes back green, the landing still
completes at the same merge sha. It is not dropped as `sprint head moved`.
The sprint push sends that merge. The runner then replays the story-file
commit on top of the merge, so the sprint branch's first-parent line shows
the landed merge and then the story-file commit. The commit's new sha
replaces the old one in the story's recorded file commits, and a
`landing-carried` event pairs the old and new shas.
