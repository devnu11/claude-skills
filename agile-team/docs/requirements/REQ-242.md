---
id: REQ-242
title: Bookkeeping commits after a sprint's green head don't make the END boundary push or open a new PR
status: active
covers: ["s188/AC1"]
---

At a sprint END boundary, commits made after the sprint's pushed head that
change only files under the team dir (`.team/`), such as a storyless step's
`chore(team): <role> step` commit, are not new work. When the replay onto the
target changes nothing else, the runner pushes no `-r<k>` branch, closes no
PR and opens none, and doesn't wait for CI again: the green PR stays the
sprint's PR and the boundary finishes green on it. The bookkeeping commits
stay on the team branch and ship with the next sprint.
