id: REQ-120
title: Runner names and PO-facing texts say Product Backlog or Sprint Backlog, never plain backlog
status: active
covers: ["s156/AC6", "s156/AC7"]
---

Hand-over triggers, the sprint-end module's names and texts, the stall lines,
the run_role refusal for a story outside the sprint, the revert notice and the
PO tools' descriptions say "Product Backlog" or "Sprint Backlog" (in names,
`product_backlog` or `sprint_backlog`), never plain "backlog". `ready_backlog`
becomes `select_ready_product_backlog`. The dashboard's Backlog view and the liaison's
`--place backlog` value are out of this rule's scope. A test pins this.
