---
id: REQ-131
title: A story's steps, scopes, hooks, commands and story-file reads run in its worktree
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC1.2"]
---

Every step on a story runs in its worktree. So do its file scopes and the
guard's scope base, the Stop hook, the step's commands and stop checks, and
the reads of the story file. The shared run files (`state.json`, the events,
the ledger, the messages and `live.json`) stay in the main checkout's
`.team/run/`.
