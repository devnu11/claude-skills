Done story s159's switch still behaves as REQ-160 says. At a sprint start
with story branches off, `start_sprint` refuses and lists, together, every
obstacle that holds:

- a story of the sprint being started is past design;
- `.team/pending-tests.json` names a story with pending tests;
- the upstream stack still has entries after the start dropped the ones
  main already has;
- the old team branch has a commit main lacks even as a patch-equivalent
  copy (`git cherry` lists a `+`). A commit whose patch main already has
  is not an obstacle.

A refused start leaves story branches off. A Product Backlog story already
past design does not block the switch, keeps its step, and gets its branch
and worktree when its next step starts.
