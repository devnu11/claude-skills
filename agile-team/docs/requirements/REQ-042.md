---
id: REQ-042
title: "The PO's role file says it schedules stories, never writes them, and asks the liaison with request_story"
status: active
covers: ["s136/AC1"]
---
The Product Owner's built-in role file describes the PO as the role that
schedules stories, not one that writes them. It says that the liaison writes
every story and the runner commits it and tells the PO its id, that the PO
never writes or edits a story file, and that the PO asks the liaison for a
story with `request_story(summary, evidence)`. No sentence in it tells the PO
to write a story file or to call `open_story`.
