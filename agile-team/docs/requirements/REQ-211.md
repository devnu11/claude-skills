---
id: REQ-211
title: A branch a story rebase step moved is put back, and the step bounces naming it
status: active
covers: ["s176/AC3"]
---

When a story rebase step starts, the runner records the head of every local
branch that the step could move: every branch except its rebase branch and
those checked out in another worktree. A step that is cut off and resumed
keeps the record from its first start. When the step ends, whatever its
handoff, the runner compares those heads again. Each branch that moved is put
back to its recorded sha, and the step bounces a round with a reason that
names each such branch with its old and new sha.
