---
id: REQ-097
title: A Board card's wait pill shows only the wait reason, and the detail shows once, on the waits-on line
status: active
covers: ["s150/AC1", "s150/AC2", "s150/AC4"]
---

A story card on the Board that waits shows a pill with the wait reason only, such as
`⏸ cancel requested`, `⏸ held` or `⏸ round cap`. The pill's tooltip is the full wait
title, reason and detail. The detail itself shows once on the card, in the `waits on:`
line, which is cut at 140 characters and has its full text in its tooltip. Node-harness
tests pin this with a 300-character cancel reason.
