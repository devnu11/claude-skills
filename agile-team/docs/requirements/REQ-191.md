---
id: REQ-191
title: A resume removes only finished, out-of-sprint and unknown worktrees and unpinned scratch dirs, one event each
status: active
covers: ["s179/AC2"]
---

`start --resume` removes a story worktree only when its story is done,
cancelled, not in the current sprint, or not in the run's state at all, and
never while the story has a step in flight. It removes the `land` and
`replay` scratch directories unless a boundary replay is pinned to
`replay`. Each removal writes one `worktree-pruned` event naming the
worktree (and the story, for a story worktree) and the reason. A removed
story worktree's recorded head is cleared, and its branch stays.
