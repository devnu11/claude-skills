---
id: REQ-150
title: revert_story drops an unlanded story's branch and reverts a landed story's merge through a landing
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC3.3", "s159/AC3.5"]
---

`revert_story` on an unlanded story drops its branch, with no revert commit.
On a landed story, from any sprint, it reverts the story's merge with
`git revert -m 1` in the land worktree. The revert lands like a story,
through the full checks, a landing branch and CI. If the revert conflicts or
its checks or CI are red, the sprint branch is untouched, and the refusal
names the failures and the later landed stories that touch the same files. A
revert of a story that a started story depends on is refused, naming that
story.
