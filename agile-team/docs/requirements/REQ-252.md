---
id: REQ-252
title: The PO moves a sprint story with no commits and no landing to the Product Backlog, and the sprint and the story's step stay
status: active
covers: ["s189/AC1"]
---

The PO calls `remove_from_sprint(story, reason)` on a story in the running
sprint that has no commits and no landing. The story leaves the sprint and is in
the Product Backlog: `agile-team status` and the Board show it there. The sprint
keeps its number, goal and branch, and no boundary, Manager turn or PO renewal
runs. The story keeps its step, status, rounds, dependencies, developer, branch
and its place in the order. Its worktree, if it has one, is removed and its
branch is kept, as for a story a new sprint leaves out.
