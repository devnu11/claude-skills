---
id: REQ-253
title: Taking a story out of the sprint clears its holds and names the reason and the cleared hold in the event and the story file
status: active
covers: ["s189/AC1"]
---

When `remove_from_sprint` moves a story, the runner's hold on it (a crash, a
gate hold, an overlap) and the PO's own hold are cleared. The story file gets a
line under `## Left the sprint` with the date, the sprint, the reason and the
cleared hold, committed by the runner in one commit. One `story-admin` event
names the tool, the reason, the sprint, the cleared hold and the record commit,
and the human gets one `update` note saying the same.
