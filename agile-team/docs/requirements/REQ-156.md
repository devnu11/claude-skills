title: A due boundary waits for the running steps, and the sprint-start Manager review blocks no story
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC6.7", "s159/AC6.8"]
---

When a sprint boundary is due, no new step starts, but PO turns go on. The
boundary runs once no step is running. The sprint-start Manager review runs
alongside the stories' steps and blocks none of them.
