---
id: REQ-149
title: A story that leaves the sprint keeps its branch, and only a story at land can't be moved or cancelled
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC3.1", "s159/AC3.2", "s159/AC3.4"]
---

A story moved out of the sprint keeps its branch. Its worktree is removed and
rebuilt from the branch when it starts again. A cancelled story's branch is
kept, unlanded, and nothing of it reaches the sprint branch. Deleting either
branch is the human's choice. An unlanded story can be moved or cancelled at
any step. A story at `land` can't be moved or cancelled until `land` ends.
