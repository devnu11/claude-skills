title: Gates, the quality step and both boundary checks run house-rules over the repo and route its failures
status: active
covers: ["s157/AC2", "s157/AC3"]
---

House-rules over the whole repo is part of the toolchain's `static` check. A
new failing finding in a file another role owns, found when a gate runs, goes
to that file's owner as a gate-fix step, as lint findings do. A warning is
never routed. The quality step and the sprint-start and sprint-end checks run
it too. A failure at the sprint end goes to DevOps's team-fix step, as other
end-check failures do.
