---
id: REQ-248
title: A story whose branch name is taken anywhere else holds with a reason that gives the way out, and continue_story starts it once that is taken
status: active
covers: ["s190/AC1", "s190/AC2"]
---

When the taken name's tip is not the sprint head, the step doesn't crash and
the runner changes nothing. It logs a `story-branch` event with
`action: "taken"`, and the story holds as refused. The reason names the branch,
its tip, the expected base (the sprint head) and the way out: a
`git branch -m <branch> <branch>-kept-<tip>` rename by the human or the
liaison, then `continue_story`. The PO's hand-over carries the reason. Once
the rename is done, `continue_story` lets the story's next step fork its
branch from the sprint head as usual.
