---
id: REQ-229
title: agile-team status shows done and the PO's reason after a held done ends the run, and shows a held done
status: active
covers: ["s184/AC3"]
---

After a run ends on the PO's `done`, held or not, re-pinned or not,
`agile-team status` reports `done` with the first line of the PO's note as
`status_reason`. It does not report `running` or `blocked`. While a done is
held, `status` shows the held reason as `held_done`.
