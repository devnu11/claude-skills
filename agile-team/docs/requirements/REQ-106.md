title: The review-stack guide says who writes the bug story for a failed CI
status: active
covers: ["s138/AC4"]
---

The customer guide to reviewing the stack says what happens when CI fails on
the first push under the current story flow. The Product Owner asks the liaison
for a bug story, the liaison writes it, and the runner opens it first in the
next sprint. The guide no longer says "the team opens a bug story".
