status: active
covers: ["s136/AC3"]
---
`agile-team/SKILL.md` and `agile-team/reference/protocol.md` each describe
the `story new` and `story amend` commands, that the runner onboards a queued
story between steps at a safe point, the authorship rule (who authors the
`docs(sN): …` commit and its `Human-involvement:` line, by `--approved-by`),
and the events the flow logs (`story-opened`, `story-amended`,
`story-refused`, `story-request`). Neither file tells anyone to write a story
file by hand or to call `open_story`.
