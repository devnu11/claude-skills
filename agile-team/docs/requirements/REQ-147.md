---
id: REQ-147
title: No push replaces a branch's history; each names its expected sha and is checked to be a fast-forward or a creation
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC2.16"]
---

Every push the runner makes names the exact sha it expects on the remote,
taken from its own record, or "absent" for a new branch. Before any push with
an expected sha, the runner checks that the new sha descends from it, and
refuses the push without sending it if it doesn't. The only exception is the
landing-branch deletion. A bare `--force-with-lease` and `--force` are never
used. A rejected push is reported to the liaison and never retried with
another expected sha.
