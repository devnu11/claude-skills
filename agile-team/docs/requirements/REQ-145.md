---
id: REQ-145
title: A boundary held on failing or missing CI follows a fix pushed to its branch
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC2.14"]
---

While a boundary holds on `ci_failed`, `ci_infra` or `ci_pending`, the runner
checks its branch's remote head on each heartbeat. When the head moved, it
fetches and fast-forwards the local branch, the stack entry and the guard's
record to it, re-enters the CI stage and clears the hold. If it can't
fast-forward, it halts as checkout drift naming both heads.
`continue_boundary` on `ci_failed` is accepted once the head has moved, and
refused while it hasn't. `record_ci_bug` is unchanged.
