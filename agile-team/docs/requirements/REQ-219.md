id: REQ-219
title: The story rebase's commits refusal lists each extra, missing or changed commit and who made it
status: active
covers: ["s182/AC3"]
---

When a story rebase's commits don't match the old branch's by subject and in
order, the bounce reason lists each extra, missing and changed commit on its
own line, with its short sha and subject. An extra or changed commit says
whether the runner or the role made it.
