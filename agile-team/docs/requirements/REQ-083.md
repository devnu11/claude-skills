---
id: REQ-083
title: A sprint-end rebase conflict is aborted, leaves the team branch unchanged and goes to a DevOps step
status: active
covers: ["s151/AC6.1"]
---
When the sprint-end rebase onto the target stops on a conflict, the runner
aborts it, so the team branch and the recorded hashes are unchanged, and runs
a DevOps step whose brief names the stopping commit, its subject and its
conflicting files, the scratch worktree and the scratch branch. The boundary
does not hold, and nothing asks the human to rebase by hand.
