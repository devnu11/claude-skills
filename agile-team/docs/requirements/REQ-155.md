Every hand-over trigger is either per-story or run-wide, in one table. The
new triggers are `story_ci_red`, `worktree_drift` and `land_bounced`. A
per-story trigger queues for the PO, and only its story waits. A run-wide
trigger stops new steps from starting. So do the budget ceiling and a
checkout halt. A trigger missing from the table fails on load.
