---
id: REQ-167
title: Stories waiting to land go in the PO's order, then smallest first
status: active
covers: ["s163/AC1"]
---

Stories still land one at a time. The stories waiting behind the one landing
go first in the order the PO set with `order_landings(stories, reason)`, then
the rest smallest first: fewest lines added plus deleted since the story's
base, ties by story id. The tool is refused for a story that isn't waiting to
land or is landing now, and for an order that departs from smallest first
without a reason. The order and its reason are recorded in an event. A story
whose landing holds or bounces leaves the queue and rejoins by the same rule
when it is ready again. A landing revert takes its turn in the same queue.
