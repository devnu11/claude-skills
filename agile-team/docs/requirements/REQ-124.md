---
id: REQ-124
title: A changed or retired requirement needs a reason in the handoff, or its edit is held back and the step bounces
status: active
covers: ["s2/AC2"]
---

The handoff may carry `requirement_changes`, a map from requirement ids to
reasons. A changed or retired requirement with no reason is not committed.
Its edit is held back in a commit that is reverted, and the step bounces as
a bad handoff with "explain change to REQ-003". An added requirement needs
no reason. Before that happens, the architect's stop is refused while a
reason is missing, so it can add the reason in the same step.
