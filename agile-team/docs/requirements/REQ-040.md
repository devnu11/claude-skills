---
id: REQ-040
title: An amended story gets its new ACs in one recorded commit and goes back to design
status: active
covers: ["s135/AC4", "s135/AC6"]
---

`agile-team story amend <id> <file> --approved-by human|none` checks and
queues a draft the same way `story new` does. At a safe point the runner
replaces the story's ACs in one commit recorded against the story, with the
same authorship rule. It sends the story back to design, where its new ACs
must be traced to requirements again. It logs a `story-amended` event with
the old and the new ACs, and tells the PO. Tests pin an amend going back to
design.
