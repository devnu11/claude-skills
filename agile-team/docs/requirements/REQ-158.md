---
id: REQ-158
title: Each story branch is pushed to its own draft PR, and its CI results are story-ci events
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC8.1", "s159/AC8.2", "s159/AC8.3"]
---

Once permission is given, each story branch is pushed after every step that
commits, to its own draft PR against the sprint branch. Landing closes that
PR. Leaving the sprint leaves it open as a draft. Each CI result on a story's
PR or its landing branch is a `story-ci` event on the story. A red result on
the story's PR hands over as `story_ci_red` and holds nothing. Only the
landing branch's CI decides whether a story lands.
