id: REQ-014
title: In Story scope the PO's box shows the time of the turns on that story
status: active
covers: ["s113/AC3", "s113/AC4"]
---

In Story scope, the Product Owner's box shows the time of the PO turns that
touched the chosen story. A turn that touched several stories counts in full
in each of them, and the box then says "shared turns". With no such turn the
time reads `—`. Tests pin a turn with no story and a turn touching two
stories.
