---
id: REQ-237
title: No story branch is forked from the sprint head while a landing runs
status: active
covers: ["s180/AC1", "s180/AC2"]
---

While a land or a landing revert is running, a story step whose story has no
worktree yet doesn't start. It waits with "waits for the running landing
before forking its branch", because forking its branch then would base it on
a commit the landing may still carry onto its merge and replace. Stories
that already have a worktree, storyless steps, the story intake and shares
don't wait.
