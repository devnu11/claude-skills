---
id: REQ-249
title: A taken story branch's commits stay reachable from a branch the event or the hold names
status: active
covers: ["s190/AC3"]
---

The runner never moves, renames or deletes a branch that already has a
story's fork name. After the start, the old tip is still reachable: from the
adopted branch, which the `adopted` event names, or from the aside branch
that the `taken` event and the hold's reason name, once the way out is taken.
