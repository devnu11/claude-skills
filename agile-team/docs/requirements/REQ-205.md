---
id: REQ-205
title: A landing that raises leaves its traceback in crash.log
status: active
covers: ["s175/AC2"]
---

When a landing ends in an exception, the runner appends the traceback to
`.team/run/crash.log` under a timestamped header, the same way it does for a
role step that crashes. The PO's hand-over says "details in
.team/run/crash.log", and that is then true for a landing too.
