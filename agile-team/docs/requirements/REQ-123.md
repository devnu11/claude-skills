---
id: REQ-123
title: A step's requirement files are sorted into added, changed and retired from git's name-status
status: active
covers: ["s2/AC1"]
---

When a step ends, the runner looks at the requirement files the step changed
in its write scope (`<docs>/requirements/REQ-*.md`). It sorts each one by its
`git diff --name-status` letter against HEAD. A new or untracked file is
added. A removed file, or one whose status changed to `retired`, is retired.
Any other edit is changed. The stories each requirement covers, before and
after the step, are recorded with it.
