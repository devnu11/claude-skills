---
id: REQ-216
title: The dashboard's live panel shows each open chore on its own row and drops it when the chore ends
status: active
covers: ["s181/AC3"]
---

The header's live panel shows one row for each open chore in the heartbeat,
naming its story, what it does and how long it has run. The rows come from
the heartbeat on every render, so a chore's row is gone within one beat of
its end. The dashboard's snapshot fixture carries the heartbeat's `chores`
list with at least one row.
