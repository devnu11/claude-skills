---
id: REQ-173
title: The sprint branch's records match the remote after a push or a follow
status: active
covers: ["s163/AC8"]
---

After a successful sprint-branch push, the stack entry records the pushed
merge before the main checkout moves. After the runner follows a remote that
moved, the entry's head and pushed sha both equal the remote head. So the
next landing's lease names the sha the remote has.
