---
id: REQ-062
title: The PO picks what the team needs with pick_commits, under the same checks and with no accept step
status: active
covers: ["s141/AC2.1"]
---
The PO tool `pick_commits(commits, reason)` asks for a pick of a commit or a
range the team needs. It runs the same checks as the CLI and is refused in
the same way. A request that passes them is applied at the next safe point,
with no accept step.
