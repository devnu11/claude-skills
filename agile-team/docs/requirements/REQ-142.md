---
id: REQ-142
title: A landing merge conflict goes back to DevOps or the developer as a bounce, and land runs again
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC2.10"]
---

A conflict in the landing merge goes back to the story as a step and hands
over as `land_bounced`. It counts as a bounce. The step goes to DevOps when
only files that no pipeline role but DevOps owns conflict, and to the story's
developer otherwise. Once that step is accepted, `land` runs again.
