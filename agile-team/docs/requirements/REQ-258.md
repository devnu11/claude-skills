---
id: REQ-258
title: A gate fix that hands off done with no commit doesn't count, runs again with a note, and repeated goes to the Manager
status: active
covers: ["s187/AC2"]
---

A gate fix step that hands off `done` but leaves no commit is not a fix. The
story does not go back to the failing step and counts no round. The same fix
stays at the head of the story's queue, and the gate event says the role's
fix changed nothing. The next brief for that fix tells the role its last
step changed nothing and that the items still stand. When this happens twice
in a row, the repeat rule escalates the story to the Manager with that
reason. A gate fix step that does commit is settled as before: its fix
leaves the queue, and after the last fix the failing step runs again.
