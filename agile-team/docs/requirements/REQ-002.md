id: REQ-002
title: One command checks the requirements and shows the coverage
status: active
covers: ["s1/AC2", "s1/AC7"]
---

`agile-team requirements check` prints JSON: every problem in the requirement
files (a broken file, a `covers` entry naming an unknown story or AC, a
duplicate id), and for every story with a story file, which requirements
cover each of its ACs. It exits 1 when there is a problem, so CI or a person
can rely on it.
