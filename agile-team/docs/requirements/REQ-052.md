---
id: REQ-052
title: A pick is applied between steps as one cherry-pick commit, and a conflict changes nothing
status: active
covers: ["s141/AC2", "s141/AC5"]
---
At the next safe point between steps, never while a sprint boundary is open,
the runner cherry-picks the commit (`git cherry-pick -x`) onto the team
branch as one new commit and rewrites no existing hash. It records the
outcome and logs a `main-pick` event naming the commit, the reason and the
new commit. On a conflict it aborts and leaves the branch where it was, then
tells the PO and the liaison which files conflicted.
