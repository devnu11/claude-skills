---
id: REQ-190
title: Architects briefed in parallel are offered requirement ids no other open work holds
status: active
covers: ["s178/AC1"]
---

With story branches, the "next free id" in the architect's brief is an id that
no requirement file uses on the sprint head, on any open story's branch or in
its worktree, and that no other open story has reserved. The runner reserves a
block of ids for the story when it writes the brief, so two architects briefed
at the same time are offered different ids.
