---
id: REQ-190
title: A resume keeps the worktree of every open sprint story, ignored files included
status: active
covers: ["s179/AC1"]
---

After a stop and `start --resume`, an open story in the current sprint
(not done or cancelled) whose worktree directory exists keeps it, whether
or not a step was in flight. The directory and its git-ignored files, such
as `house-rules/runner/.venv`, are still there when the story's next step
starts, and that step runs no `git worktree add` for it.
