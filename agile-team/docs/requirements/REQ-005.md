title: The dashboard shows the requirements and what they cover

The dashboard's Requirements tab lists each requirement with its status and
the story ACs it covers, and shows which ACs are traced. When there are no
requirement files, the tab says so in one line; once one exists, that line is
gone and the list shows instead.
