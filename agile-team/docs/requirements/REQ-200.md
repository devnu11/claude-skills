---
id: REQ-200
title: The runner's own commits made during a landing are carried over its merge, which lands at its own sha
status: active
covers: ["s180/AC1", "s180/AC2"]
---

While a landing's merge waits on its checks or its CI, the runner may still
commit on the main checkout: a story filed or amended, a shared file, a
story-admin record, a storyless step's commit or a PO settle. When the
landing's CI comes back green and only such plain commits moved the sprint
head since the merge was made, the landing doesn't give up its merge. It
lands at the same merge sha: the sprint branch on the remote moves to the
merge, and on the main checkout the moved commits are replayed on top of the
merge, in their order, with their authors and messages. No `landing-dropped`
event is written for it. The run's records that named a moved commit name
its new copy, and the `story-landed` event lists each old and new sha.

The landing still gives up its merge, as before, when the move can't be
carried: another merge moved the head, the head doesn't descend from the
merge's first parent, the remote already has a moved commit, a story branch
starts on a moved commit, or replaying a moved commit conflicts.
