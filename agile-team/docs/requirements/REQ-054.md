id: REQ-054
title: The PO is told after a pick, never asked first, and its role file says what picks are for
status: active
covers: ["s141/AC4", "s141/AC5"]
---
After the runner settles a pick, the PO gets a `main_pick` hand-over line so
it can plan around the pick. It is never asked for approval first: a pick the
human or the liaison queued is applied with no PO turn in between. The PO's
role file says picks are for a fix or rule already on main that the team
needs now, never for whole sprints of work.
