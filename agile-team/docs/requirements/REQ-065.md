---
id: REQ-065
title: A pick is applied between steps, one cherry-pick commit per source commit, is recorded, and a conflict changes nothing
status: active
covers: ["s141/AC5.1", "s141/AC5.2", "s141/AC5.3"]
---
At the next safe point between steps, the runner cherry-picks (`-x`) each
commit of an accepted, forced or own pick, oldest first. Each becomes its own
commit on the team branch, and no existing hash changes. A `pick` event
records the commits and the new shas, the reason and who asked, how the pick
was decided (accepted, forced or the PO's own), and whether each source
commit is on `<remote>/<branch>`. When any commit conflicts, the whole pick
is aborted, the branch is left as it was, and the files are reported to the
PO, the liaison and the human.
