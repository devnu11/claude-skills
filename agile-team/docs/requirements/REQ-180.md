---
id: REQ-180
title: house-rules refuses a suppression whose reason only says tests or a design fixed the name
status: active
covers: ["s161/AC2"]
---

house-rules reports a `suppression-excuse` failure for each
`# house-rules: allow` comment whose reason only says that tests, a test
file or a design fixed the name. The finding gives the file, the line and
the rule, and lists each matching reason. The patterns are house-rules
defaults, and a repo adds its own with the `excuses` setting in
`.house-rules.toml`. A suppression with any other reason is reported as
before.
