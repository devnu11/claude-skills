id: REQ-063
title: The PO accepts or denies a pick request, and an unanswered one stays pending and visible
status: active
covers: ["s141/AC3.1", "s141/AC3.2", "s141/AC3.3"]
---
`accept_pick(request)` accepts a pending request, and the runner applies it
at the next safe point. `deny_pick(request, reason)` denies it for any
reason: a `pick-denied` event is recorded, the reason goes to the liaison and
the human, and the branch doesn't change. A request the PO hasn't answered
stays pending and shows in `agile-team status` and on the dashboard.
