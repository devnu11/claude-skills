---
id: REQ-117
title: Under until-blocker, done and blocked are refused only while a Sprint Backlog story can move
status: active
covers: ["s156/AC3", "s156/AC7"]
---

Under cadence `until-blocker`, the runner refuses the PO's `notify_user` with
kind `done` or `blocked` while a story in the current sprint can move: it is
active, not held, waits on no unmet dependency or open question, and no boundary
is pending. The refusal names those stories. Ready Product Backlog stories never
cause the refusal. A test pins this.
