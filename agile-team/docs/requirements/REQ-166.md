---
id: REQ-166
title: Before starting a step the runner reserves budget for every running step
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC12.1"]
---

Before starting a step, the runner adds a reserve for every running step. It
starts none if spend plus the reserves could reach the budget ceiling. A
step's reserve is the larger of mean + 3σ and `[team] step_reserve_usd`,
taken over the fresh, completed steps of that role and model in the ledger.
Below 5 such steps, only the floor applies. `step_reserve_usd` defaults to
1.00 and must be at least 0 and below `budget_usd`. No running step is ever
stopped for cost.
