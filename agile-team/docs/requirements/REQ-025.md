---
id: REQ-025
title: Tests compare durations with a tolerance
status: active
covers: ["s129/AC3"]
---

A test that checks a duration (seconds, slept, awake, age and the like) for
equality allows a small tolerance and never compares exactly. A scan of the
test tree fails on an exact equality compare of a duration in an assert,
including inside tuples, lists and dict literals.
