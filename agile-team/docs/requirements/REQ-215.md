---
id: REQ-215
title: The heartbeat lists every open chore with its story and since
status: active
covers: ["s181/AC2"]
---

`heartbeat.json` has a `chores` list in place of the single `chore` value.
It holds one row per open chore, oldest first, and each row has `what`,
`story` (the story id, or `null` for run-wide work such as a sprint boundary
or the PO waiting for answers) and `since`. With no open chore, or once the
runner has ended, the list is empty.
