---
id: REQ-013
title: Each PO turn records the stories it touched
status: active
covers: ["s113/AC2", "s113/AC4"]
---

Every `po-turn` event lists the stories the PO's tool calls named during that
turn (`run_role`, `continue_story` and any other PO tool that names a story),
once each, and an empty list when the turn named none. Tests pin a turn with
no story and a turn with two.
