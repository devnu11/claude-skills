---
id: REQ-220
title: A Manager advance is refused while the story's rebase is queued
status: active
covers: ["s182/AC3"]
---

A Manager `advance` ruling on a story with a queued story rebase is reported
as not applied, with a reason that names the rebase and the rulings that do
apply. The story's step does not move, so the same rebase is never checked
again under a later step's name. Other rulings still apply.
