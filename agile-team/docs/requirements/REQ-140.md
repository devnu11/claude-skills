---
id: REQ-140
title: The hash remap pairs commits story by story and rewrites base shas, landed merges and revert merges
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC2.7"]
---

After a replay, the runner pairs the old and new commits story by story. It
pairs each story's commits by subject, in order, and each merge by its
`merge(<id>)` subject. The remap also rewrites each open story's base sha,
each landed story's merge, and the merge shas in the revert log.
