title: With story branches on, a resume keeps every open sprint story's worktree and removes only finished, out-of-sprint and unknown ones
status: active
covers: ["s185/AC1"]

Done story s159's resume still behaves as REQ-160 says after s179 changed
it. With story branches on, after a stop and `start --resume`:

- the worktree of every open story in the current sprint (not done or
  cancelled) is kept, whether or not a step was in flight;
- the worktree of a story with an interrupted step is always kept, whatever
  its status or sprint;
- the worktree of a story that is done, cancelled, not in the current sprint
  or not in `state.json` is removed, with exactly one `worktree-pruned` event
  each, naming the worktree and its reason.

A kept worktree logs no event. This is a re-check of REQ-193 and REQ-194
from s159's side, with one story of each kind in one resume.
