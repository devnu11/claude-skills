---
id: REQ-195
title: A kept worktree that drifted is reported at its story's next step, not removed by the resume
status: active
covers: ["s179/AC3"]
---

A resume never removes a worktree because it drifted. A kept worktree whose
HEAD differs from the recorded `worktree_head`, that is on another branch,
or that has a git operation in progress is reported as drift when its
story's next step starts, holding only that story, as before.
