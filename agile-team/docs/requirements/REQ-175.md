title: After a resume, the cut-off role runs again in the kept worktree with its session, and its brief says why
status: active
covers: ["s165/AC2"]
---

After a resume, the next step on a cut-off story is the same role's, at the
step the story was on. Its brief says that role's step was cut off, names the
cause, and says the uncommitted edits are still in the worktree. The step runs
in that worktree, with those edits in place, and resumes the role's kept
session.
