---
id: REQ-028
title: A step's pass record is committed before the story moves on
status: active
covers: ["s131/AC1", "s131/AC3"]
---

When a gate passes on a step that has a pass record (today only
`ops-review`, whose record is the story file's `## Tooling` section), the
runner writes the record and commits it before the story moves to the next
step. A crash between the two can no longer leave a story that has moved on
but has no record. Tests pin this order.
