---
id: REQ-141
title: At landing the story branch's story file wins, the sprint's story-admin edits are re-applied, and a clash goes to the PO
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC2.9"]
---

When a story lands, its story file is the story branch's copy, with the
story-admin edits made on the sprint branch while the story was open
re-applied on top. If the two clash, the landing holds and the PO is told.
