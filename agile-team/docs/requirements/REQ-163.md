---
id: REQ-163
title: A usage limit stops new steps, lets each running one fail alone, and ends the run with status limit
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC11.4"]
---

When the account's usage limit is hit, nothing is cancelled. Each step that
hits it fails on its own and is marked interrupted, and no new step starts. A
landing already waiting on CI finishes its wait. When no step is running, the
status is `limit`, with the reset time as its reason, and the run exits 75. A
PO turn cut off by the limit keeps its session, and its hand-overs stay
queued.
