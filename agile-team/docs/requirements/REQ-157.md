id: REQ-157
title: Each role's session on a story is kept and resumed until a listed event ends it, and resumed steps are ledgered as deltas
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC7.1", "s159/AC7.2", "s159/AC7.3"]
---

Each role's agent session on a story is kept, and resumed on that role's
next step on the same story. A kept session ends when:

- the story is done or cancelled;
- `revert_story` runs;
- a bounce sends the story back;
- an override changes the role's model.

A session past the context limit is compacted. If compaction fails, the next
step starts fresh. A resumed step's cost is the session's delta. The step
event and the ledger entry record whether it resumed, and the step event
records its cache-write tokens. The Time chart can split fresh steps from
resumed ones.
