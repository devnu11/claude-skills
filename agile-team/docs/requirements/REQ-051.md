---
id: REQ-051
title: The human, the liaison and the PO can ask for one commit from main, and bad asks are refused
status: active
covers: ["s141/AC1", "s141/AC5"]
---
The human or the liaison asks with `agile-team pick <commit> --reason
"<text>"`, and the PO asks with the `pick_from_main(commit, reason)` tool.
Both check the ask at once, after a fresh fetch, and queue it for the runner.
The runner checks it again before it applies it. A pick is refused with a
reason, and nothing changes, when the reason is blank, there is no upstream,
the value isn't a commit, the commit isn't on `<remote>/<branch>`, it is a
merge commit, or its change is already on the team branch. The PO can't veto
or reorder a pick the human or the liaison queued.
