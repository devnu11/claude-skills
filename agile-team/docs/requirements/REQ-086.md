---
id: REQ-086
title: An unresolved conflict or failing checks hold the boundary and reach the PO and the liaison with the scratch branch
status: active
covers: ["s151/AC6.4"]
---
When DevOps does not finish, its result is refused, or the full checks fail
in the worktree, the boundary holds `rebase_conflict`. The PO and the
liaison get the stopping commit, its files, the reason, DevOps's notes and
the scratch branch's name; the scratch branch is kept. The team branch is
unchanged. `continue_boundary` starts a fresh rebase and DevOps attempt.
