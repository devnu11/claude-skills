---
id: REQ-255
title: Taking out a story with commits, a landing or an open landing is refused, changes nothing and points to revert_story
status: active
covers: ["s189/AC3"]
---

`remove_from_sprint` refuses a story that has commits, has landed, or is
landing (at `land`, in the landing slot, or with a revert landing queued). The
refusal says to use `revert_story`, and nothing changes: no state, event, story
file line or commit. It also refuses, with nothing changed, an unknown, done or
cancelled story, a blank reason, a story not in the running sprint, a story
waiting on a cancel or revert question, a story whose step is running, and a
story unfinished sprint stories depend on.
