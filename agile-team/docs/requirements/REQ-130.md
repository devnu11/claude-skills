---
id: REQ-130
title: Each story gets its own branch and a worktree inside the repo, made from the sprint head
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC1.1"]
---

When a story's first step starts, the runner makes the story branch
`<story_prefix><id>` from the sprint branch's head and records that head as
the story's base sha. The story's worktree is `.team/run/worktrees/<id>/`,
inside the repo and gitignored. The runner creates nothing outside the repo
except under the system temp dir. For a step in a worktree, the guard's scope
base is that worktree, and the git ceiling stays at `.team/run`.
