id: REQ-153
title: Up to max_open_stories stories have worktrees, and their steps run at once, one per story
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC6.1", "s159/AC6.2"]
---

`[team] max_open_stories` (default 5, an integer of at least 1) caps how many
stories have a worktree at once, and no new story starts beyond it. Steps on
different open stories run at the same time, one step per story. Each
story's hand-over is gathered on its own. The live meter and the health
check track one entry per running step. Acceptance sandboxes are keyed by
story and environment. The budget is checked before each start. `stop`
finishes every running step, and `stop --now` cancels them all.
