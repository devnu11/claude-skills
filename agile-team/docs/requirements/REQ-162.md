---
id: REQ-162
title: A run waiting on the human stays running, says why, and carries on within seconds of an answer
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC11.3"]
---

A run that can't move because it waits on the human doesn't exit. That covers
an open question, a hold, the push permission, the stuck and keep-going caps,
and the PO's `notify_user(kind=blocked)`. Its status stays `running` with the
reason, and it keeps its sessions. When `UNATTENDED_HEARTBEAT` is set, the
runner writes it on each beat, with `waiting: <reason>` while it waits. It
carries on within seconds of an answer or a tell. The deadline still stops it
through the stop command, and `notify_user(kind=done)` still ends it.
