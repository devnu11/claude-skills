title: The PO's box on the Flow chart shows its awake time and its waiting
status: active
covers: ["s113/AC1", "s113/AC4"]
---

In Sprint and Run scope, the Product Owner's box on the Flow chart shows the
PO's awake time (its model time plus its own tool time) in the same format as
every role box, and on its own line the time it spent waiting on the roles it
ran. Tests pin both against fixture events.
