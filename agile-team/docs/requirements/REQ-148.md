id: REQ-148
title: A sprint start creates the sprint branch on the remote and the sprint's stack entry
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC2.17"]
---

A sprint start creates `<prefix><N>` on the remote at the start head, under
the push permission. It also makes the sprint's stack entry with its start,
head and pushed sha. Story branches fork from it, and their draft PRs target
it. The end boundary reuses this entry.
