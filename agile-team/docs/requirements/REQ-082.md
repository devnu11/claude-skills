`README.md`'s agile-team row, `agile-team/SKILL.md`,
`agile-team/reference/protocol.md` "Sprint boundaries" and
`.team/roles/_leadership.md` describe the sprint end as: push the team
branch as it is, wait for green CI, rebase onto main, push again to the same
PR, wait for CI again. None of them says the team merges main or builds
stacked or cherry-picked review branches.
