---
id: REQ-093
title: The team branch moves to a resolved sha only while it is still at the expected head
status: active
covers: ["s153/AC2"]
---
`Upstream.adopt` moves the team branch to a given sha and returns each team
commit paired with its new copy, but only while the team branch is still at
the head the caller expects. When it has moved, `adopt` refuses with
`TEAM_MOVED` and resets nothing.
