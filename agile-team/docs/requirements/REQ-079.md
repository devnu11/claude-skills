id: REQ-079
title: After green, the team branch is rebased, re-pushed to the same PR, and its CI is recorded without holding
status: active
covers: ["s151/AC3.1", "s151/AC3.2", "s151/AC3.3"]
---
After the as-built CI is green, the runner rebases the team branch onto
`<remote>/<branch>` locally and rewrites the recorded hashes, as the
`rebase` stage always has. It force-pushes the rebased branch to the same
`<prefix><N>` with a lease on the as-built sha, so the PR updates and its
history keeps the as-built push. It waits for that CI and records the
result. A green result makes the sprint done. Any other result is reported
to the PO and the liaison as "NOT ready", and the boundary still finishes.
