---
id: REQ-144
title: An unlanded story carried into a sprint is replayed onto the new sprint head on a new branch
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC2.13"]
---

At a sprint start, each story carried over without landing is rebased from
its base sha onto the new sprint head, on a new branch
`<story_prefix><id>-r<k>`. An interrupted step's uncommitted edits are
stashed first and put back after. The story's base and shas are remapped,
its pushed head resets, and its draft PR is replaced by one from the new
branch. The old branch is kept, and so are its kept sessions. A conflict
becomes that story's next step, and the boundary goes on.
