---
id: REQ-152
title: Stories start after their dependencies land, and land one at a time
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC5.1", "s159/AC5.2"]
---

A story with `depends_on` starts only once every story it depends on has
landed. Stories land one at a time: a story reaching `land` while another is
landing waits for it. The order they land in is REQ-167's (s163 replaced the
acceptance order of s159 AC5.2).
