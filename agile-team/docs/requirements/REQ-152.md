---
id: REQ-152
title: Stories start after their dependencies land, and land one at a time in acceptance order
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC5.1", "s159/AC5.2"]
---

A story with `depends_on` starts only once every story it depends on has
landed. Stories land in the order they passed acceptance. A story reaching
`land` while another is landing waits for it.
