---
id: REQ-030
title: A running role's box on the Flow chart counts its open step
status: active
covers: ["s137/AC1", "s137/AC3"]
---

While a role works, its box on the Flow chart adds the running step to the
scope's totals: the step's tokens and dollars so far (the same turns its
tokens/min rate comes from) and its awake time so far. This holds in Story,
Sprint and Run scope whenever the running step is inside the scope, and the
numbers grow with each snapshot. A role on its first step of a story never
reads `0 · $0.00 · 0s` in Story scope. Every other role's box is unchanged.
Tests pin this against fixture events: non-zero numbers for a running first
step in Story scope, numbers growing between two snapshots, and an idle role
unchanged.
