---
id: REQ-177
title: The architect names what a design adds by the house rules, and a refused name goes back to design
status: active
covers: ["s161/AC3"]
---

The architect's role file says that every function, method, class and
module a design adds gets a name the house rules accept: a verb first, and a
no-argument query as a property. The developer's and the unit-tester's role
files say that a design name the rules refuse goes back to the architect, as
a `changes_requested` handoff with `next_role: architect` that names the
function and the rule. The developer or tester neither renames it nor
suppresses the rule.
