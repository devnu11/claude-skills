---
id: REQ-035
title: The shared-files brief section gets its story id from its caller
status: active
covers: ["s133/AC3", "s133/AC4"]
---

The `## Shared files` section of a brief names the story that its caller
passes in, together with the role. No code works out a story id from a
shared file's name. A test pins that the story id passed in is the one shown,
even when a file's name starts with another id.
