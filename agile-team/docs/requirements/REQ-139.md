title: A replay conflict goes to DevOps's scratch replay, checked story by story
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC2.6"]
---

A conflict during a replay goes to a DevOps step in the scratch replay
worktree, which replaces the scratch rebase. The brief names the story whose
commits stopped. The runner's check of DevOps's result compares the story
merges and each story's commits, story by story. It has no refusal of
merges.
