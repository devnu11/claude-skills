---
id: REQ-160
title: The switch to story branches happens at a sprint start only when nothing is in the way
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC10.1", "s159/AC10.2", "s159/AC10.3"]
---

The runner switches to story branches at a sprint start only when all of
these hold:

- no Sprint Backlog story is past design;
- `pending-tests.json` is empty;
- the upstream stack is empty once the start has dropped the entries main
  already has (REQ-170);
- the old team branch has no commit that main lacks, even as a
  patch-equivalent copy (`git cherry` lists no `+`).

Otherwise it refuses and names every obstacle. An older `state.json` loads
with no story-branch fields. `start --resume` removes only the worktrees
REQ-194 lists, and keeps every open sprint story's (REQ-193) and every one
whose story has an interrupted step. A Product Backlog story already past
design keeps its step and gets its branch and worktree when it next starts.
