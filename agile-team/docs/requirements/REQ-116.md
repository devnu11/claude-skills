id: REQ-116
title: A Sprint Backlog stuck for 3 hand-overs with no PO action on its stories blocks the run and tells the liaison
status: active
covers: ["s156/AC2", "s156/AC7"]
---

The runner counts its stuck Sprint Backlog hand-overs. A step that runs, or a PO
call that isn't refused to `continue_story`, `release_story`, `run_role`,
`pass_tests`, `revert_story`, `request_cancel` or `request_story`, resets the
count, as do `start_sprint` and `add_to_sprint`. When the Sprint Backlog is
still stuck after 3 hand-overs, the runner sets the run `blocked` instead of
handing over. The reason says "the Sprint Backlog is stuck" and gives each
story's stall reason. The runner posts the same text to the liaison as a
`blocked` note. This holds under both cadences. A test pins this.
