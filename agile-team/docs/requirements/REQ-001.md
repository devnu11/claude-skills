---
id: REQ-001
title: Requirements are structured files
status: active
covers: ["s1/AC1", "s1/AC7"]
---

Each requirement of the product is one file, `<docs>/requirements/REQ-NNN.md`.
Its frontmatter holds the id (the file's name), a title, a status (`active` or
`retired`) and the story ACs it covers, written `sN/ACk`. Its body says the
requirement in plain words. Reading the files names every broken file and
what is wrong with it, and no two files share an id.
