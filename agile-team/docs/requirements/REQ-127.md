---
id: REQ-127
title: Stories past design that trace a changed or retired requirement go back to design with no round, and done ones reach the PO
status: active
covers: ["s2/AC5"]
---

When a step changes or retires a requirement, every other open story that
the requirement covers and that is past design goes back to design. No round
is counted, and the story gets a note naming the change. Done stories that
the requirement covers are listed as `affected_done_stories` in the step's
report. The PO is handed them so it can ask the liaison for a follow-up
story.
