---
id: REQ-247
title: A story whose branch name is taken at the sprint head runs on that branch, with an event that says why
status: active
covers: ["s190/AC1"]
---

When a story with no branch record starts and a local branch already has the
name its fork would take, the runner doesn't crash. If that branch's tip is
the sprint head, the runner takes it as the story's branch, with no new
commit, and runs the step in a worktree on it. A `story-branch` event with
`action: "adopted"` names the branch and its base and says why the branch was
used.
