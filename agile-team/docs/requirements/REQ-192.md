---
id: REQ-192
title: A dropped landing names every commit that moved the sprint head
status: active
covers: ["s180/AC3"]
---

When a landing gives up its merge because the sprint head moved, its
`landing-dropped` event lists every commit between the merge's first parent
and the new sprint head, oldest first, each as its short sha and subject,
and says why the move could not be carried. The land step's report carries
the same list, and the PO's hand-over for that story names the dropped merge
and those commits, so nobody has to run git to see what moved the head.
