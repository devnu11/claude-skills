---
id: REQ-192
title: A developer step on a story whose PR CI is red gets the failing checks in its brief
status: active
covers: ["s172/AC3"]
---

While a story's latest PR CI result is red, every developer step on that
story gets a `## Story CI` section in its brief. The section names the commit
and lists the same failing checks, steps and lines as the PO's hand-over, so
nobody retypes them and the developer doesn't ask for the log. Other roles
don't get the section, and neither does a story whose latest CI is green or
comes from its landing.
