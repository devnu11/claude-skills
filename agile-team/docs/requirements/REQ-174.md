---
id: REQ-174
title: The scheduler's own writes never wake it; writes from anywhere else do
status: active
covers: ["s164/AC1"]
---

While a run waits, the scheduler ticks at most once per `POLL_S` (5 s).
Whatever the scheduler task writes for itself on a tick, such as its
`checkout.guard` stage event or its own state saves, is written before it
waits and does not end the wait early. A write queued by any other task (a
step, a PO turn, a tool call, a boundary stage, a story CI poll) or by a
worker thread still ends the wait at once.
