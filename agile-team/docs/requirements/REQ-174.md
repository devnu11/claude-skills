---
id: REQ-174
title: A run resumes after a story step was cut off, and records that step's role, story, brief and cause
status: active
covers: ["s165/AC1"]
---

When a run stops while a story step runs in its worktree (a crash, a kill, a
reboot), `agile-team start --resume` starts. The cut-off step ends as
`stopped`. The story keeps its worktree, with any uncommitted edits, and the
step's session. The story's `interrupted` record in `state.json` names the
step's role, the story, the step's brief and the stop's cause, each in its own
field.
