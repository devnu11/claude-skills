---
id: REQ-174
title: An unattended job report says how long the job has left before its deadline
status: active
covers: ["s162/AC1"]
---

`unattended status`, `unattended list` and `unattended wait` give each job a
`time_left` right after its `deadline`. It is the whole minutes until the
deadline, rounded down, written the way `limits.span` writes durations
(`23h41m`, `41m`), and `0m` under a minute. Once the deadline has passed it
is `overdue`, and for a job with no deadline it is `null`.
