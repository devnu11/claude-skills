---
id: REQ-268
title: A refused sprint push whose remote already equals the merge counts as pushed, and the story lands
status: active
covers: ["s177/AC2"]
---

When the sprint push is refused but the fetched remote head equals the merge
the runner tried to push, the push went through and only its reply was lost.
The landing goes on exactly as after a successful push: the stack entry's
`pushed` and `head` become the merge, the main checkout fast-forwards to it,
and the story is recorded landed with `ci: green` (`story-landed` event).
No `landing-dropped` event is logged and no new merge is made.
