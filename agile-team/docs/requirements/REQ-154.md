Within the runner, one task applies every change to `state.json`, the events
and the ledger. Step tasks and PO tool calls hand it their changes. Requests
from other processes stay files it reads. The live meter writes its own
files.

The PO gets hand-overs from several stories, queued and given one turn at a
time. A turn reads a snapshot taken when it starts. Each tool call is applied
against the live state, and refused with the story's new state if the story
has moved since. Hand-overs that arrive during a turn wait for the next one.
