---
id: REQ-267
title: A refused sprint push holds at once with its refusal text when the remote didn't move, and keeps its merge for the retry
status: active
covers: ["s177/AC1"]
---

When the sprint push is refused and the fetched remote head of the sprint
branch still equals the runner's record (`pushed`), the landing holds at
once. The hold reason is `pushing the sprint branch <branch> was refused:
<refusal text>`. No new merge is made. The story keeps `landed.merge` and
`landed.pushed`, its landing branch stays on the remote, and no
`landing-dropped` event is logged. `continue_story` or `release_story` runs
the land again, which resumes that same merge (its CI read again at the same
sha) and pushes it again.
