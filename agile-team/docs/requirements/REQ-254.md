---
id: REQ-254
title: A story taken out of the sprint no longer stops the sprint end
status: active
covers: ["s189/AC2"]
---

After `remove_from_sprint` moves a story, the sprint-end check no longer names
it. When every other story of the sprint is done, cancelled or reverted, the
PO's `sprint-end` note is not refused, the end boundary opens, and under
until-blocker a `done` or `blocked` is not refused because of that story. The
sprint-end refusal and the stuck Sprint Backlog advice name
`remove_from_sprint` for a story with no commits.
