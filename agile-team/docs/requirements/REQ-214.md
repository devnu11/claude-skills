---
id: REQ-214
title: A chore leaves the heartbeat when its own block ends, in whatever order overlapping chores end
status: active
covers: ["s181/AC1"]
---

Each runner chore is listed in `heartbeat.json` from the moment its block
starts until that same block ends. When chores from two stories overlap and
end in any order (A starts, B starts, A ends, B ends), the heartbeat shows
only B once A has ended and no chore once B has ended. A chore that has
ended is never shown again, and ending one chore never brings back or
removes another.
