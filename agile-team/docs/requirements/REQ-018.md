---
id: REQ-018
title: A CI job that never ran a step is re-run once by the runner
status: active
covers: ["s111/AC4", "s111/AC6"]
---

A failed or cancelled job that ran no step (GitHub never ran it) is
infrastructure, not our code. The runner re-runs it once for the pushed head,
and it counts only when that re-run is green on the same commit. If it still
runs no step, the boundary holds `ci_infra` and the runner does not re-run it
again. A test pins one re-run and no second one.
