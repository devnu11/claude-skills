---
id: REQ-269
title: A landing gives up "the sprint branch kept moving" only after five distinct remote heads, and names them
status: active
covers: ["s177/AC3"]
---

When a refused sprint push finds a remote head that descends from the
runner's record but is neither the record nor the merge, the landing gives
up its merge, follows the remote and lands again on the new head, as before.
Only the remote heads followed this way count toward the limit: after
`MAX_RESTARTS` (5) distinct ones in one land, the land holds with `the sprint
branch kept moving: its remote moved 5 times, to <head>, <head>, ...`, each
head as its first 12 characters, oldest first. A fresh start for another
cause (a carry the landing refused) doesn't count toward this limit; it has
its own limit of `MAX_RESTARTS` and its own hold reason.
