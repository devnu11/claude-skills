---
id: REQ-235
title: A storyless step's commit made while a landing waits on CI doesn't cost that landing its merge
status: active
covers: ["s180/AC2"]
---

A storyless step, such as a DevOps config change run with an empty story,
can commit on the main checkout after a landing was pushed and before its CI
comes back. The landing then completes at its merge sha, the same way as for
a story-file commit (REQ-234). The landing waits for the step to finish,
because the step holds the main checkout's lock. The step's commit then
follows the landed merge on the sprint branch.
