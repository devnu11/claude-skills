---
id: REQ-126
title: Each landed requirement change is a requirement-changed event with its id, kind, reason and commit
status: active
covers: ["s2/AC4"]
---

For every requirement change a step commits, the runner writes a
`requirement-changed` event to `.team/run/events.jsonl`. The event has the
requirement id, the kind of change (added, changed or retired), the reason
and the commit's sha. A held-back change writes no event.
