id: REQ-085
title: A clean DevOps rebase with green checks becomes the team branch, remapped, and the sprint end goes on
status: active
covers: ["s151/AC6.3"]
---
When DevOps's result passes the runner's checks and the full checks, the
runner moves the team branch to it (only if the team branch has not moved),
remaps the recorded hashes as for a clean rebase, removes the worktree and
the scratch branch, and goes on to push the rebased branch to the same
sprint PR.
