---
id: REQ-135
title: Landing merges the story into the sprint head with one --no-ff merge, keeping the story's commits
status: active
covers: ["s159/AC2.1", "s159/AC2.2"]
---

After a story's acceptance passes, the runner checks out the sprint head,
detached, in the scratch worktree `.team/run/land/`. There it runs
`git merge --no-ff` of the story branch with the message
`merge(<id>): <title>`, so the sprint head is the first parent. The worktree
is removed after each landing. This is the only kind of merge commit the
runner makes. The story's own commits keep their hashes.
