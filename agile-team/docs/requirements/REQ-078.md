---
id: REQ-078
title: Green CI on the as-built push gates the rebase; red holds and is reported to the PO and the liaison
status: active
covers: ["s151/AC2.1", "s151/AC2.2"]
---
The runner waits for the CI of the as-built push. The team branch is
rebased only after that CI is green on the pushed head. A red result holds
the boundary, and the PO and the liaison both get the failing checks. With
no CI result at all (no `gh`, no permission, or no checks), nothing is
rebased at the sprint end, and the report says so.
