id: REQ-064
title: Only the human forces a pick, confirmed by typing its sha, and the PO is told once it is applied
status: active
covers: ["s141/AC4.1", "s141/AC4.2", "s141/AC4.3"]
---
`agile-team pick … --force` skips the PO's decision. It works only on an
interactive terminal: the command shows the commits, their subjects and the
team branch, and the human must type the first commit's short sha. With no
terminal, or a wrong answer, it refuses and queues nothing. Once a forced
pick is applied, the PO gets a `pick-forced` hand-over with the commits, the
reason and the new shas.
