---
id: REQ-066
title: The PO reverts an accepted pick or its own in one commit, never a forced one
status: active
covers: ["s141/AC6.1", "s141/AC6.2"]
---
`revert_pick(pick, reason)` reverts an accepted pick, or one the PO asked for
itself, in a single revert commit. It is recorded with an event and reported
to the liaison and the human. A forced pick is the human's, and
`revert_pick` refuses it.
